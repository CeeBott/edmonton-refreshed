Common Questions

Where can I view pieces?

All available pieces are in Edmonton. Viewings are by appointment — no set hours. Text or call 780-965-1477 and we’ll arrange a time.

What brands do you carry?

We specialize in premium makers: Natuzzi, B&B Italia, La-Z-Boy, Rove Concepts, Brentwood Classics, Crate & Barrel, Urban Barn, EQ3, American Leather, and similar. If it’s a recognized brand in good condition, we’re interested.

Do you deliver?

Delivery is available at an additional fee determined by distance to your home from our location, and complexity of delivery - are you in an apartment? Or is your desired room located anywhere other than the main floor of your home? This is the main factor determining complexity, but not the only one.

How do I sell my sofa or sectional?

Head to the Sell Your Furniture page. Send photos, the brand, age, and your asking price — we respond within 24 hours with a fair offer and handle all pickup.
