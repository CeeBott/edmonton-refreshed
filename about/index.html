About Edmonton Refreshed

A curated pre-owned furniture operation based in Edmonton, focused on well-built sofas and sectionals from recognized makers.

What We Do

Edmonton Refreshed launched in April 2025. We source quality pre-owned sofas and sectionals from Edmonton homeowners, estate sales, interior designers, and staging companies. Every piece is evaluated in person, assessed for condition, cleaned, and priced fairly before being listed.

The focus is strictly on well-built pieces from recognized makers — the kind of furniture that holds its quality over time and is worth owning second-hand. We don’t list every sofa we come across; we list the ones we’d be comfortable buying ourselves.

Over 39 sofas and sectionals have been purchased and placed into Edmonton homes since we started. The 4.9/5 rating across 16 buyer reviews reflects an approach built on accurate descriptions, fair pricing, and straightforward transactions.

Our inventory has included pieces from Italian, Canadian, and American manufacturers known for quality construction and design. Past and current brands include:

Our Process

Every piece we list has been seen, evaluated, and described by us directly — not from a photo sent by a third party. Condition notes in listings reflect what we actually found, including any wear, marks, or imperfections worth disclosing. Each piece is one of a kind. Once it’s gone, there’s no guarantee we’ll get another one like it.

Pricing is based on original retail value, brand, age, and current condition. We research comparable listings and price to reflect genuine value — not wishful thinking.

Viewings are by appointment in Edmonton. Pieces can be inspected in person before any commitment. We answer questions about construction, materials, and brand history because knowing the furniture matters when you’re buying it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I view pieces?

All available pieces are in Edmonton. Viewings are by appointment — there are no set hours. Text or call 780-965-1477 and we’ll arrange a time that works.

What brands do you carry?

We focus on well-built pieces from recognized makers: Natuzzi, B&B Italia, La-Z-Boy, Rove Concepts, Brentwood Classics, Crate & Barrel, Urban Barn, EQ3, American Leather, and similar. If it’s a well-built piece from a known manufacturer, we’re likely interested.

Do you deliver?

Delivery is available at an additional fee determined by distance to your home from our location, and complexity of delivery - are you in an apartment? Or is your desired room located anywhere other than the main floor of your home? This is the main factor determining complexity, but not the only one.

How do I sell my sofa or sectional?

Text or email photos along with the brand, approximate age, and your asking price. We respond within 24 hours. If we proceed, we handle pickup at your convenience and pay cash or e-transfer on the spot. See the Sell Your Furniture page for details.

What condition does a piece need to be in?

No major damage, significant staining, or structural problems. Normal wear consistent with age is fine — we assess each piece honestly and price it accordingly. We’re transparent about condition in every listing.

How do you set prices?

Based on original retail value, brand, age, and current condition. We research comparable listings and price to offer genuine value without overpaying or underpricing. Descriptions include what you’d pay new so you can judge the savings yourself.

Do you serve areas outside Edmonton?

Yes. We regularly work with buyers and sellers from St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan, as well as all areas of Edmonton.
