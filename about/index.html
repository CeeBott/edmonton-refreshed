About Edmonton Refreshed

A curated pre-owned furniture operation based in Edmonton, focused on well-built sofas and sectionals from recognized makers.

Meet Collin

I’m Collin Bottrell. I started Edmonton Refreshed in April 2025, and I still run every part of it myself.

I didn’t come from the furniture world. I spent six and a half years in the auto industry in sales and service, ran a residential painting business for a stretch, and most recently managed social media for a group of small-cap mining companies. I wanted to build something of my own that I could stand behind directly — and the more time I spent buying and selling secondhand, the clearer the gap got: plenty of people want genuinely well-built furniture but can’t justify new prices, and even more want the value you can find on Marketplace without the Marketplace experience.

My first sale was a teak dresser set I’d inherited from my grandmother — it sat in my basement for years before I refinished it and sold it. I don’t deal in much of that anymore. Piece by piece, I found my niche: high-quality sofas and sectionals in excellent condition.

Everything I know about this furniture, I’ve learned by doing — handling the brands, researching how they’re built and what they’re made of, and pricing every buy and sale against my own sold data. I don’t have a design degree or a showroom. What I have is real time with these pieces and a track record I can point to.

I’m picky about what I take on, so my inventory runs lean and there are slow stretches. I’d rather that than list something I wouldn’t buy myself. Secondhand furniture is rarely perfect unless it’s nearly new, so I tell you exactly what you’re getting: I clean every piece, rotate and plump the cushions, condition the leather, and disclose the flaws as plainly as the features. I answer quickly, I stay reachable, and I try to make the whole thing easy.

I was born and raised in Edmonton. I run the business here because I love this city and I’m betting on where it’s headed.

What I Do

I source quality pre-owned sofas and sectionals from Edmonton homeowners, estate sales, interior designers, and staging companies. Every piece is evaluated in person, assessed for condition, cleaned, and priced fairly before being listed.

The focus is strictly on well-built pieces from recognized makers — the kind of furniture that holds its quality over time and is worth owning second-hand. I don’t list every sofa I come across; I list the ones I’d be comfortable buying myself. The reputation I’m building rests on accurate descriptions, fair pricing, and straightforward transactions.

How I Work

Every piece I list has been seen, evaluated, and described by me directly — not from a photo sent by a third party. Condition notes in listings reflect what I actually found, including any wear, marks, or imperfections worth disclosing. Each piece is one of a kind. Once it’s gone, there’s no guarantee I’ll get another one like it.

Pricing is based on original retail value, brand, age, and current condition. I research comparable listings and price to reflect genuine value — not wishful thinking.

Viewings are by appointment in Edmonton. Pieces can be inspected in person before any commitment. I answer questions about construction, materials, and brand history because knowing the furniture matters when you’re buying it.

Where I Operate

I regularly work with buyers and sellers throughout the greater Edmonton area, including St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I view pieces?

All available pieces are in Edmonton. Viewings are by appointment — there are no set hours. Text or call 780-965-1477 and we’ll arrange a time that works.

What brands do you carry?

We focus on well-built pieces from recognized makers: B&B Italia, Natuzzi, Rove Concepts, Crate & Barrel, EQ3, American Leather, Urban Barn, Brentwood Classics, and La-Z-Boy. If it’s a well-built piece from a known manufacturer, we’re likely interested.

Do you deliver?

Yes — delivery is available across Edmonton and the surrounding area for an additional fee. The cost depends on distance and on access at your end — stairs, elevators, and tight corners all add time. Tell us where the piece is headed and we’ll confirm the delivery cost up front.

How do I sell my sofa or sectional?

Text or email photos along with the brand, approximate age, and any condition notes. We get you an offer today. If we proceed, we handle pickup at your convenience and pay cash or e-transfer on the spot. See the Sell Your Furniture page for details.

What condition does a piece need to be in?

No major damage, significant staining, or structural problems. Normal wear consistent with age is fine — we assess each piece honestly and price it accordingly. We’re transparent about condition in every listing.

How do you set prices?

Based on original retail value, brand, age, and current condition. We research comparable listings and price to offer genuine value without overpaying or underpricing. Descriptions include what you’d pay new so you can judge the savings yourself.

Do you serve areas outside Edmonton?

Yes. We regularly work with buyers and sellers from St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan, as well as all areas of Edmonton.
