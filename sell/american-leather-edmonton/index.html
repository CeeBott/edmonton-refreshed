Sell Your American Leather Sofa or Sectional in Edmonton

American Leather is a Texas-based manufacturer with one of the best build reputations in the North American premium upholstery market. The brand is most recognized here for the Comfort Sleeper line and the Tuscany stationary sofa — both of which we actively look for.

Get an Offer

What we look for from American Leather

American Leather pieces are built in Dallas with consistent quality control and a wide range of top-grain and full-grain leather options. The Comfort Sleeper is the line’s standout — a sleeper sofa that genuinely sleeps like a bed, without the metal bar. Comfort Sleepers in working mechanical condition carry strong pre-owned values regardless of age.

For stationary upholstery, Tuscany, Henley, Westchester, and the Quick Ship leather configurations all qualify. American Leather’s leather grades range widely: top-grain is the floor, and the upper end includes full-aniline and semi-aniline that develop genuine patina.

Mechanism state is the single biggest factor with this brand. A Comfort Sleeper with a non-functional mechanism is a fraction of the value of one that opens and closes cleanly. Same for any electric headrest or recliner mechanisms — we test these in person before finalizing.

What affects the offer

  • Mechanism state: Comfort Sleeper and reclining models must have all working motors and a smooth open-close action.
  • Leather grade: top-grain holds value well; full-aniline and semi-aniline are at the upper end.
  • Model: Comfort Sleeper, Tuscany, Henley, and Westchester lead the line.
  • Age: a working Comfort Sleeper from 10 years ago can still be in our buy zone — mechanical state outweighs age.
  • Configuration: matching ottoman or chair pairs add value; mismatched orphans don’t.

Pieces that typically qualify

  • Comfort Sleeper in any size (Twin XL, Full, Queen, King) with a working mechanism.
  • Tuscany sofas and sectionals in any top-grain or higher leather configuration.
  • Henley, Westchester, and Whitman sofas in good or better condition.
  • Quick Ship leather pieces in neutral or rich colourways.

    Common questions

    Does it matter if mine is a sleeper sofa or a standard sofa?

    Both qualify. The Comfort Sleeper is a core American Leather product and is evaluated the same way as a stationary sofa — condition, leather grade, and overall state. The mechanism just needs to function.

    Do you buy American Leather in fabric as well as leather?

    Yes. American Leather makes fabric-upholstered pieces alongside their leather line, and we consider both. Leather tends to hold higher pre-owned value, but a fabric piece in good condition still qualifies.

    Does the leather grade change the offer that much?

    Yes. The same model in full-aniline can offer at twice what the top-grain version offers, all else equal.

    What condition issues affect the offer most?

    Surface cracking or peeling on the leather — most common on armrests and seat fronts — strong odours, and structural issues like broken frames or sagging seat decks. Minor surface wear is normal and factored in; it’s rarely disqualifying on its own.
