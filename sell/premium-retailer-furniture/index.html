Sell Furniture From Scandia, McElheran’s, F2, and Other Edmonton Stores

Updated October 2026 · Hosted by Collin Bottrell, Edmonton pre-owned furniture buyer.

To sell furniture you bought at an Edmonton store like Scandia, McElheran’s, or F2, send us photos for a no-obligation offer. Edmonton Refreshed buys sofas and sectionals from the city’s premium retailers, and from the brands they carry, outright: we pick the piece up and pay you before it leaves. If you’re replacing it, we’ll aim to time pickup around your new delivery.

Below, store by store: what each one carries, according to its own website, and which of those pieces we buy. You don’t need to know who made your piece. The store you bought it from is a good start.

    Selling furniture from Scandia, McElheran’s, F2, and other Edmonton stores

    Each store below is described from its own website, checked in October 2026. Where a store carries a brand we buy, that brand’s page has more detail.

    • Scandia Furniture. Scandia sells Natuzzi Italia alongside other contemporary European furniture. We buy Natuzzi Italia sofas and sectionals.
    • McElheran’s Furniture + Design. Its brands page lists American Leather, along with makers like Bernhardt, Century, and Hancock & Moore. We buy American Leather, and sofas and sectionals from recognized makers like these fit what we look for.
    • F2 Furnishings. F2’s brands page lists EQ3 and Natuzzi Editions, both brands we buy. If you’re replacing a piece, one line in F2’s FAQ is worth knowing: “Please note that we do not remove and dispose of existing furniture.” If your new piece is coming from F2, the old one needs its own way out, and we’ll aim to pick it up before your delivery.
    • Cottswood Interiors. Its brands page lists American Leather, a brand we buy, along with makers like Lee Industries, CR Laine, and Vanguard.
    • Signature Lane. Signature Lane’s website lists Van Gogh Designs, a brand we buy, and Signature Lane itself is named in our buying criteria.
    • LightForm and Dwell Modern. LightForm’s furniture page lists Cassina, Ligne Roset, and Knoll. We buy Knoll, and seating from makers like Cassina and Ligne Roset is worth sending in.
    • Reside Furnishings. Reside’s website features Stressless sofas and recliners. If you have a Stressless sofa, send photos and we’ll tell you whether it’s a fit.
    • CosaFina. Its brands page lists Verellen and Hickory Chair. Sofas from recognized makers like these fit what we buy when they meet the basics below.
    • Crate & Barrel and RH. If you bought from a brand’s own store, its page has the detail: selling a Crate & Barrel piece or selling an RH piece.

    Store policies change, and we can only speak for ourselves. If you’d like a trade-in, ask the store you’re buying from. If it doesn’t offer one, or you’d rather have cash, the trade-in alternative is the same as everything on this page: send photos and get a direct offer.

    Is my piece a fit?

    Pieces that typically qualify

    • Sofas and sectionals from the stores above, including unbranded pieces from Signature Lane
    • Brands those stores carry, like American Leather, Natuzzi, EQ3, Van Gogh, Knoll, Cassina, and Verellen
    • Pieces roughly 10 years old or newer
    • Leather or fabric, in good condition

    Pieces we don’t typically purchase

    • Dining tables, beds, storage, lighting, and decor, even from these stores
    • Standalone chairs, unless exceptional
    • Mass-market brands: IKEA, Ashley, Leon’s, The Brick, or similar
    • Bonded or faux leather, and microfiber recliners
    • Major damage, pet damage, or staining

    If you’re not sure where yours falls, send photos and we’ll tell you straight.

    Finding out who made your piece

    Many pieces sold by Edmonton’s premium stores carry the maker’s label, even when the store’s name is the one you remember. A few places to look:

    • Under the seat cushions. Lift each cushion and check the deck and the cushion zippers for a label or tag.
    • Underneath the frame. Tip the piece back carefully, or check the dust cover on the underside. On American Leather pieces we’ve looked at, the label there shows a frame code, a build date, and the name of the store that ordered it.
    • Your receipt or order confirmation. It usually names the maker and the model, and shows when you bought it.

    If you can’t find anything, send what you have. The store, a few photos, and roughly when you bought it are enough to start.

    What affects our offer
    • The maker, or the store if the maker isn’t known. Recognized brands and premium retailers resell most reliably.
    • Age. A receipt or a dated label helps here.
    • Condition. Leather or fabric, cushions, and frame, as they are today.

    Selling privately or selling to us

    The honest version: a private sale usually pays more if you have the time, and our offer comes in below it. What you get from us is a firm number, pickup handled for you, and payment before the piece leaves.

    • Private sale: often the highest price, with time and the right buyer. You handle the listing, messages, viewings, and getting it out of the house.
    • Selling to us: a firm offer, below a private sale, paid by cash or e-transfer before pickup. You send photos, and we handle the rest.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do Edmonton furniture stores buy back used furniture?

    We can only speak for ourselves, so ask the store you’re buying from whether it takes trade-ins. If it doesn’t, or you’d rather have cash than store credit, send us photos of the piece for an outright offer.

    Will F2 Furnishings take away my old sofa when it delivers a new one?

    F2’s own FAQ says: “Please note that we do not remove and dispose of existing furniture.” If your new piece is coming from F2, send us photos of the old one. If it’s a fit and you accept our offer, we’ll aim to pick it up before your delivery.

    Do you buy furniture from Scandia, McElheran’s, Cottswood, or Signature Lane?

    Sofas and sectionals from these stores are the kind of pieces we buy, as long as they’re roughly 10 years old or newer and in good condition. Send photos for an offer. Not every piece gets one, and we’ll tell you plainly either way.

    I know where I bought my sofa but not who made it. Can I still sell it?

    Yes. Put the store’s name in the Brand field, and add a photo of any label you find under the seat cushions or on the underside. The receipt helps if you still have it, but it isn’t required.

    Can you pick up my old piece before my new one is delivered?

    Often, yes. Tell us the delivery date when you send photos. If you accept our offer, we’ll aim to schedule pickup so the old piece is gone before the new one arrives.

    Do you pay cash or store credit?

    Cash or e-transfer, paid before the piece leaves your home. There’s no store credit, so the money can go toward a new piece from any store.
