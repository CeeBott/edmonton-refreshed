Sell Your Leather Sofa in Edmonton

Pre-owned leather sofas from quality brands are one of the most consistent things we buy. Leather ages slowly, retains structural integrity well, and has a buyer base that’s actively shopping the secondary market in Edmonton year-round.

Get an Offer

Why leather sofas resell so well

A new top-grain leather sofa from a recognized brand starts at $3,000 CAD and runs well past $10,000 for premium models. Buyers searching at that price point have read enough to know that a well-maintained pre-owned piece in good leather is often a stronger value than a new entry-tier sofa — same retail dollar buys more sofa pre-owned.

The brands we focus on for leather: Natuzzi Italia and Editions, B&B Italia, American Leather, Brentwood Classics (especially Dorado in full-aniline), Roche Bobois, Restoration Hardware, Crate & Barrel (leather lines), Urban Barn (top-grain only), and EQ3 (Remi). La-Z-Boy leather is also accepted selectively at the higher-end models.

A note on leather condition: minor surface wear, light patina, and small scuffs are usually fine — in many cases they’re what buyers actively prefer over over-protected, plasticky-looking finishes. What does affect offers: cracking, peeling, significant staining, ink marks, and pet damage. Photos help us evaluate accurately.

What affects the offer

  • Leather grade: full-aniline and semi-aniline lead; top-grain is the floor; bonded and faux leather are generally out of scope.
  • Brand: premium European, American premium, and Canadian premium leaders dominate the offer range.
  • Colour: cognac, espresso, charcoal, and black are the strongest resellers; bright or saturated hues are softer.
  • Surface state: minor wear is acceptable; major cracking or peeling significantly reduces offers.
  • Frame and cushion: solid hardwood frames with intact spring systems and shape-retaining cushions support the strongest offers.

Leather sofa types we look for

  • Top-grain or higher leather sofas from Natuzzi, B&B Italia, American Leather, Restoration Hardware, Brentwood Classics.
  • Full-aniline leather sofas in any size or configuration — we actively look for these.
  • Reclining leather sofas with working mechanisms (Greg, Boavista, Comfort Sleeper, etc.).
  • Loveseat and chair sets in matching leather — sold together with the sofa.
  • Vintage leather sofas in solid structural shape — especially Italian and Scandinavian designs.

    Common questions

    My leather is faded on one side from the sun — is the piece still worth anything?

    Probably yes, but with a reduced offer reflecting the fading. Send photos so we can assess how visible it is in person.

    Should I condition the leather before selling?

    Not necessarily. We clean and inspect every piece we take in. Don’t risk a product reaction by trying to condition aged leather right before pickup.

    Is genuine leather different from real leather?

    "Genuine leather" is actually a specific grade in the leather hierarchy — the lowest tier of real leather, made from split-grain. "Real leather" usually means top-grain or full-grain. The distinction matters for pricing — genuine-grade leather sofas are less resaleable than top-grain.

    My sofa is leather on the front and vinyl on the back — is that a fit?

    Sometimes — it’s called split-hide construction and is common at mid-tier brands. We evaluate case by case based on the brand and overall condition.
