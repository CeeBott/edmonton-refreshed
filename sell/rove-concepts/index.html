    What we look for from Rove Concepts

    Rove Concepts occupies a specific niche on the Edmonton resale market — design-forward, Vancouver-based, recognizably contemporary. The Milo has been one of the most popular pieces we’ve listed.

    Chatou bouclé, performance fabric configurations, and leather pieces all have a willing audience here.

    We’ve bought and resold the Milo (any configuration is welcome), and we also look for the Porter, Kaye, and Luca. Modular pieces with all original modules and full back cushions get the strongest offers; stripped-down or mismatched configurations price below that.

    Newer pieces (post-2020) are typically in strong enough shape that condition isn't a barrier, even on higher-end configurations. Older pieces (pre-2018) require a closer look at cushion deflation, fabric pilling, and leg integrity.

    Pieces we buy

    • Modular sectionals like the Milo, Porter, and Kaye
    • Luca sofas and sectionals
    • Leather configurations from any model in the line
    • Bouclé and performance fabric variants

    Does Rove Concepts buy back or take trade-ins?

    No. Rove Concepts doesn’t run a buy-back or trade-in program for used furniture. Its Trade Program and paid Members program are discounts on new orders. Its return policy covers pieces received back within 30 days, in original packaging and like-new condition, which doesn’t help with a sectional you’ve lived with for years.

    If a new Milo or Porter is on order, send photos of the current piece and tell us the delivery date. If you accept our offer, pickup is scheduled with you, and you’re paid by cash or e-transfer before we leave.

    Why Edmonton sellers contact us about a Rove Concepts piece

    The configuration doesn’t work in the new space

    Modular sectionals are room-specific. A Milo or Porter configuration that worked in the old living room may not fit the new layout when you’re selling before a move — and reconfiguring the modules doesn’t always solve it.

    New furniture is arriving and the current piece needs to go

    Upgrading to a different model, swapping fabric to leather, or moving on from the brand. Selling the current piece outright means no listing to run while you wait for the new one. If a sofa trade-in toward the new piece is what you had in mind, a direct sale gets the same job done: the outgoing Rove bought outright.

    The room is being renovated or redesigned

    A new aesthetic direction, flooring change, or full room refresh. The Rove piece is in good shape — it just no longer fits where the room is going. A design change is one of the most common reasons a well-maintained sofa moves on.

    Not worth the time to list and manage a Marketplace sale

    Rove sells online-only, which means resale buyers often can’t see the brand in person before committing. Some sellers with good pieces decide a single conversation and a scheduled pickup is worth more than waiting on Marketplace.

    What affects the offer
    • Model: the Milo, Porter, and Kaye are the Rove models we look for most
    • Configuration completeness: complete modular sets price highest, and we price whatever modules you have
    • Fabric or leather condition: bouclé in clean, unpilled shape commands a premium; pilling or staining drops the offer significantly
    • Foam compression: Milo cushions use high-density foam with a goose down layer; they're firm but the down layer shows compression over time. Permanently flattened seats affect the offer.
    • Age: 0–6 years is the strongest band; well-maintained older pieces are still considered as long as the upholstery is still in good condition without major staining, and the frame is solid and quiet

    How to evaluate your Rove Concepts piece for resale

    A few practical checks tell you whether your Rove piece is in the band where it’s worth submitting.

    • Confirm the model. Rove labels are typically inside a cushion zip or stapled to the underside of a module. The model name (Milo, Porter, Kaye, Luca) and configuration code identify the piece quickly. Original Rove order emails carry the same info.
    • Inventory the modules. Modular sectionals like Milo and Porter are configuration-flexible but priced as complete sets. Count the seat modules, back cushions, ottomans, and corner pieces. Tell us if a module is missing and we'll price what's there.
    • Check cushion shape and rebound. Milo cushions use high-density foam wrapped in a goose-down layer. The foam holds; the down compresses with use. Press into a seat — does it rebound or stay flattened? Permanent compression affects the offer.
    • Look closely at bouclé or velvet surfaces. Light bouclé pilling is normal and not disqualifying; severe pilling, snag damage, or pet damage matters more. Velvet shows directional wear and crush marks under arms or on seat fronts.
    • Confirm all original feet or legs are present. Rove feet often unscrew for delivery and are sometimes set aside in a closet during install and forgotten. Check before submitting.

    If you can’t tell from those checks, send photos of the side profile, the back-cushion top (showing fill compression), the seat surface up close, and any visible label. We identify model and configuration from photos in most cases.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does the fabric my Rove is upholstered in affect the offer?

    Yes. Chatou bouclé and performance fabric configurations tend to hold value better than lighter linens or velvets. Leather is strong across the board. Neutral colourways outperform statement tones regardless of material.

    I have a custom Rove configuration — does that change the offer?

    No. Modular layouts can be reconfigured, so a custom arrangement isn’t a problem.

    Do you take the matching ottoman with the sofa?

    Yes — we prefer to take the set together. Ottomans paired with the original sofa add to the offer and make the resale straightforward.

    My Rove piece is more than six years old — still worth sending?

    Yes. Send photos of the upholstery and seat cushions — condition carries more weight than age once a piece is past the six-year mark.

    Where can I sell my Rove Concepts sofa or sectional in Edmonton?

    Edmonton Refreshed buys Rove Concepts sofas and sectionals directly from sellers across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan, including the Milo, Porter, Kaye, and Luca. Send photos through the form on this page. We aim to reply the same day.

    Will I get more for my Rove Milo on Marketplace?

    Possibly. The Milo has been one of the most popular pieces we've listed, so a complete Milo can do well for the right seller. The honest trade is that a complete Milo sectional in good condition with strong photos can outperform our offer. What you give up is the time, the modular disassembly, and the risk of the bouclé or velvet surfaces being damaged during a private pickup.

    Do you handle Rove modular sectional disassembly?

    Yes. We've disassembled and moved modular Milo sectionals as part of pickup. The Milo's free-form modular design means no hardware to unlock, but the individual modules need to be lifted and wrapped carefully to avoid scratching the legs or marking the upholstery. Our crew handles it.

    Is there a Rove Concepts buy-back or trade-in program in Edmonton?

    No — Rove Concepts doesn’t offer a buy-back or trade-in program. But you can sell yours outright to Edmonton Refreshed — send photos for an offer, paid by cash or e-transfer with pickup handled.
