    How to decide what to sell vs. move

    If you’re not sure which pieces are worth taking to the new place and which to sell, a few practical filters help.

    • Move cost vs. piece value. Movers charge by weight or volume. A large sectional often runs into the hundreds of dollars to move within the city — and meaningfully more for interprovincial. If the move cost (plus the hassle of getting it down the stairs) approaches the piece’s resale value, selling usually wins.
    • Fit at the new place. Measure doorways, elevators, hallways, and the intended room. A sectional that won’t fit through an apartment elevator becomes a dismantling job or a return-to-curb situation on possession day.
    • Style and scale alignment. If a furniture refresh is already planned for the new home, moving the old piece just delays the decision and adds cost on both ends.
    • Condition at the destination. Older pieces sometimes don’t survive a move — frame stress, fabric snagging in transit, and warehouse storage can degrade what was a usable sofa.

    The general rule: when in doubt, send photos. You’ll have a number to compare against the move quote, and there’s no commitment in asking.

    Why Edmonton sellers contact us before a move

    Out-of-province move

    Shipping a sectional across provinces often costs more than the piece is worth on the other end. Sellers reach out when the move math doesn’t justify taking it.

    Downsizing the floor plan

    Condo, apartment, or smaller-home moves where a sectional simply won’t fit the new layout, elevator, or stairwell.

    Tight possession date

    Move day is locked in and Marketplace timelines don’t align with the calendar. Pickup gets scheduled around the date you already committed to.

    New home, different look

    The existing piece doesn’t suit the new space’s scale, style, or layout — even when dimensions would technically work.

    Why furniture gets harder to sell as the move date approaches

    Selling a large piece privately works best when you have time on your side. Eight weeks out, you can post on Marketplace, field messages over a few weekends, hold a couple of showings, and reduce the price gradually if it doesn't move. None of that is true at two weeks out.

    Buyer behaviour also shifts as your urgency becomes obvious. Listings that read “need gone before the 30th” or “moving — first reasonable offer” attract a different audience — lowballers, pickup-tomorrow promises that fall through, and a steady stream of “is this still available?” messages from people who never reply again. The closer you get to the move date, the worse the math gets on private sale.

    And the practical side: a sectional or large sofa that doesn't sell before the truck arrives becomes either a moving cost (paying movers to relocate furniture you didn't want), a storage cost, or a curbside donation. None of those are good outcomes if the piece had real value.

    Where direct purchase fits in a move

    The reason this category is one of our most active is that the trade is genuinely useful. Some price flexibility — our offer typically lands below what the same piece might fetch with eight weeks of patient listing — in exchange for a small set of guarantees:

    • An offer today, often within a few hours
    • Pickup scheduled around your possession date, with most coordinated within 72 hours
    • Truck, blankets, straps, and load-out handled on our end — no disassembly required from you
    • Cash or e-transfer at the door before the piece leaves the home
    • One conversation, not 40 Marketplace messages

    For sellers in the last few weeks before a move, that combination usually outweighs the price gap. The hours you'd spend listing, messaging, scheduling, and walking strangers through your half-packed house are worth something too.

    Selling to us vs. listing on Marketplace before a move

    The honest version: with eight weeks of runway and energy for the process, Marketplace can pay more. With three weeks until possession day, the calculation changes. The Marketplace timeline doesn't align with the moving timeline, and the friction of running both at once usually eats whatever upside the listing produces.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How quickly can you pick up before my move date?

    From the time we receive photos, the full process to pickup is typically 72 hours or less. We schedule around your possession date and can usually offer same-day or next-day pickup if your timeline is tight.

    Do I need to disassemble or move the sectional myself?

    No. We bring the truck, blankets, and straps, and handle disassembly and load-out. The piece doesn't have to leave the room until we arrive.

    What if my move is in two weeks and I haven't listed anything yet?

    That's the most common scenario we see. Send photos when you're ready — most offers go out the same day, and pickup can be scheduled in the days leading up to your move so the piece is gone before the movers arrive.

    What if I'm moving out of Edmonton — does that change anything?

    No, as long as the piece is in the greater Edmonton area for pickup. We pick up across Edmonton and surrounding communities (St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, Fort Saskatchewan).

    Can I get an offer before I commit to anything?

    Yes. Submitting photos is just the start of a conversation. You're not obligated to accept any offer we make, and there's no pressure if you decide to keep the piece, list it elsewhere, or change your timeline.

    Where can I sell furniture fast before moving in Edmonton?

    Edmonton Refreshed buys quality sofas and sectionals directly from sellers on tight timelines — offer back today, pickup scheduled around your possession date, paid by your preferred method before we leave. We cover Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan. Submitting photos through the form on this page is the fastest path to a firm number.

    Should I list my furniture on Marketplace before a move or sell direct?

    It depends on your timeline. With six-plus weeks of runway, Marketplace can pay more. With three weeks or less to possession day, the listing process usually runs longer than the calendar allows, and sellers end up either delaying the move, paying to move the piece, or scrambling for a last-minute donation slot. A direct buyout removes the timeline variable.

    Can you pick up furniture on possession day or the day before?

    Yes. We schedule pickup around your move date and routinely handle same-day or next-day pickups in the final week before possession. Just mention the date in the form and we'll work with you.
