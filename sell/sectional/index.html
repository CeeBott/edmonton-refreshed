Updated September 2026 · Hosted by Collin Bottrell, Edmonton pre-owned furniture buyer.

To sell a sectional in Edmonton, send photos to Edmonton Refreshed for a no-obligation offer. We aim to reply the same day, and if you accept, we handle dismantling, moving, and payment.

Sectionals are our most active buying category and one of the hardest pieces to sell privately. The size, the weight, and the module configurations make a Marketplace listing more trouble than it’s worth for most people.

    What we typically buy

    We focus on sectionals from design-oriented and mid-to-premium retailers: Italian and European brands (Natuzzi Italia, Natuzzi Editions, B&B Italia, Roche Bobois), American premium (American Leather, Restoration Hardware, Room & Board), Canadian mid-to-upper (Rove Concepts, EQ3, Brentwood Classics, Younger Furniture, VanGogh), and select catalogue retailers (Crate & Barrel, West Elm, Pottery Barn, Urban Barn).

    Modular sectionals separate modules that can be reconfigured — tend to be the most straightforward buy. Buyers want flexibility, and complete modular sets in their original configuration are reliably in demand. Fixed L-shaped and U-shaped sectionals are also in our buy zone, as long as the layout works for a range of room sizes rather than a highly specific footprint.

    Is my piece a fit?

    Sectional configurations we look for

    • Modular sectionals from recognized brands — Rove Concepts Milo, Restorations Hardware The Cloud, etc
    • L-shaped and U-shaped sectionals in neutral upholstery from premium retailers
    • Leather sectionals from Natuzzi, American Leather, Crate & Barrel, Brentwood Classics
    • Performance-fabric sectionals from EQ3, Rove Concepts, Crate & Barrel, and West Elm select models

    Sectionals we don’t typically purchase

    • Mass-market sectionals from Ashley, IKEA, Leon's, or The Brick
    • Bonded leather or heavily peeling configurations
    • Power-reclining sectionals with non-working motors
    • Hide-a-bed sectionals with bent frames or torn mattress bottoms
    • Heavy smoke saturation or significant pet odour issues
    • Large visible tears, severe leather cracking, or structural instability

    If your sectional falls into any of these categories, donation through Find Edmonton Furniture Bank, the Bissell Centre, or Habitat for Humanity ReStore is usually the better path. If you're unsure, send photos — we'll tell you straight.

    Why Edmonton sellers contact us about a sectional

    The sectional outgrew the room

    Family room redesign, new layout, or downsizing to a smaller home means the existing footprint doesn’t fit. We take 100+ inch pieces in a single trip.

    Marketplace dragged on for weeks

    Sectionals are one of the slowest categories to move privately. Sellers contact us after 4–6 weeks of listings with no firm buyer.

    A new sectional is on the way

    Replacement is ordered and the old configuration can’t sit in the room past delivery day. We schedule pickup around the new piece’s arrival.

    One of several pieces in a refresh

    Sectional plus matching chairs, an ottoman, or other seating going at the same time. We can take the qualifying pieces in one visit.

    What affects the offer
    • Brand and original retailer: sets the floor and ceiling of what we can offer
    • Configuration: complete modular sets with all original modules outperform partials or reconfigured pieces
    • Upholstery: leather and performance fabric hold stronger pre-owned value than light linens or velvet
    • Age and condition: 0–8 years from a smoke-free, low-pet household is our strongest range
    • Cushion shape: compressed or permanently flattened cushions affect the offer significantly

    How to evaluate your sectional for resale

    A few quick checks tell you whether your sectional is in the band where it's worth submitting.

    • Brand check. Look for the manufacturer tag — usually under a seat cushion or stapled to the underside of one of the modules. Recognized brands hold meaningful pre-owned value; mass-market brands rarely clear the threshold regardless of size.
    • Modular vs. fixed. If the sectional separates into independent modules — corner, armless, chaise, ottoman — that's typically a stronger seller because the buyer can configure for their room. A fixed L- or U-shape only works for specific footprints.
    • Connectors and frame. Check the connectors between modules — clips, brackets, or interlocking grooves should be intact and tight. A frame that creaks or shifts under weight is a meaningful deduction.
    • Cushions. Press into the seat — do cushions hold their shape and rebound? Permanently flattened or sagging cushions, particularly on non-removable seats, affect the offer.
    • Reversibility. Reversible chaises and configurable corners fit more buyer homes than fixed-direction pieces, which supports a stronger offer.
    • Upholstery and edges. Check seams, especially on chaise edges where it's easy to catch a foot. Leather peeling, fabric pilling, or pet damage all factor in.

    If most of those check out, send photos from multiple angles — including the full layout in the room — and we'll give you a straight read the same day.

    Selling a sectional to us vs. listing on Marketplace

    Sectionals are one of the toughest categories to move on Marketplace. The size narrows the buyer pool, the configuration (L vs U vs modular, facing direction, fixed vs separable) narrows it further, and the logistics of moving 100+ inches of furniture through a stranger's stairwell narrows it even more. Marketplace can still produce the highest ceiling on the right piece. What we offer is a different trade.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I measure a sectional for an accurate offer?

    Photos are more useful than measurements for an initial offer — but if you have them: overall length along each side, depth from the wall, and height at the back. Ballpark numbers are fine.

    Do I need to disassemble the sectional before pickup?

    No. We handle disassembly and load-out and bring everything needed — straps, blankets, truck.

    My sectional has a chaise that’s reversible — does that affect the offer?

    Slightly in your favour. Reversible chaises fit more room layouts, which makes them easier to place.

    I have a sectional with a hide-a-bed mechanism — does it qualify?

    Often yes, if the mechanisms works. We don’t buy sleeper sectionals with broken frames or torn mattress bottoms.

    Where can I sell my sectional in Edmonton?

    Edmonton Refreshed buys quality sectionals directly from sellers across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan. L-shaped, U-shaped, and modular configurations all qualify; we handle disassembly and load-out, and pay before we leave. Submit photos through the form on this page and we'll get you an offer today.

    Who buys used sectionals in Edmonton?

    Edmonton Refreshed buys sectionals from recognized brands — Natuzzi, B&B Italia, Rove Concepts, EQ3, Crate & Barrel, West Elm, American Leather, Restoration Hardware, Brentwood Classics, and similar. We're one of the few buyers in Edmonton focused specifically on sofas and sectionals, which is why our process is built around the logistics of large seating — not generic used-furniture handling.

    Will I get more for my sectional on Marketplace?

    Sometimes — on a popular configuration in a popular fabric, with weeks of patience. The honest trade is that Marketplace's ceiling on a sectional can exceed our offer, but sectionals are one of the slowest categories to move privately because of size, configuration, and pickup logistics. Our offer trades some price for certainty, speed, and pickup handled.
