Why Rove holds its value

Rove Concepts occupies a specific niche on the Edmonton resale market — design-forward, Vancouver-based, recognizably contemporary. Buyers looking for that aesthetic know the brand, so demand tends to be steady rather than seasonal.

Chatou bouclé, performance fabric configurations, and leather pieces all have a willing audience here.

Of the catalogue, the Milo (any configuration), Porter, Kaye, and Luca are the strongest performers. Modular pieces with all original modules and full back cushions get the strongest offers; stripped-down or mismatched configurations price below that.

Newer pieces (post-2020) are typically in strong enough shape that condition isn't a barrier, even on higher-end configurations. Older pieces (pre-2018) require a closer look at cushion deflation, fabric pilling, and leg integrity.

What affects the offer

  • Model: Milo, Porter, and Kaye carry the highest pre-owned values in the line
  • Configuration completeness: modular sectionals need all original modules; missing pieces reduce offers materially
  • Fabric or leather condition: bouclé in clean, unpilled shape commands a premium; pilling or staining drops the offer significantly
  • Foam compression: Milo cushions use high-density foam with a goose down layer; they're firm but the down layer shows compression over time. Permanently flattened seats affect the offer.
  • Age: 0–6 years is the strongest band; well-maintained older pieces are still considered as long as the upholstery is still in good condition without major staining, and the frame is solid and quiet

Pieces that typically qualify

  • Modular sectionals (Milo, Porter) with all original modules and back cushions
  • Stationary sofas (Kaye, Luca, Lyla) in good condition or better, any upholstery
  • Leather configurations from any model in the line
  • Bouclé and performance fabric variants — both consistently in our buy zone

The Milo modular sectional in particular is something buyers search for by name — the brand has strong recognition in Edmonton's modern-furniture community, and the modular configuration is the closest thing to "buyer-friendly" you'll find on a large piece. What we offer is a different trade: certainty, scheduled pickup, and modular disassembly handled.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does the fabric my Rove is upholstered in affect the offer?

    Yes. Chatou bouclé and performance fabric configurations tend to hold value better than lighter linens or velvets. Leather is strong across the board. Neutral colourways outperform statement tones regardless of material.

    I have a custom Rove configuration — does that change the offer?

    Usually not negatively. Modular layouts can be reconfigured, so a custom arrangement isn't a problem unless the upholstery is something the next buyer is unlikely to want.

    Do you take the matching ottoman with the sofa?

    Yes — we prefer to take the set together. Ottomans paired with the original sofa add to the offer and make the resale straightforward.

    My Rove piece is more than six years old — still worth sending?

    Yes. Send photos of the upholstery and seat cushions — condition carries more weight than age once a piece is past the six-year mark.

    Where can I sell my Rove Concepts sofa or sectional in Edmonton?

    Edmonton Refreshed buys Rove Concepts sofas and sectionals directly from sellers across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan — Milo, Porter, Kaye, Luca, Lyla, and others. Submit photos through the form on this page and we'll reply with a firm offer within 24 hours.

    Will I get more for my Rove Milo on Marketplace?

    Possibly — the Milo is one of the strongest pieces on the Edmonton Marketplace for the right seller. The honest trade is that a complete Milo sectional in good condition with strong photos can outperform our offer. What you give up is the time, the modular disassembly, and the risk of the bouclé or velvet surfaces being damaged during a private pickup.

    Do you handle Rove modular sectional disassembly?

    Yes — we disassemble Milo and Porter configurations routinely as part of pickup. The Milo's free-form modular design means no hardware to unlock, but the individual modules need to be lifted and wrapped carefully to avoid scratching the legs or marking the upholstery. Our crew handles it.
