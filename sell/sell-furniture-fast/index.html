    What “fast” actually means here

    Two timelines are worth understanding:

    • Offer turnaround: typically same-day. If your message lands during business hours and the photos are clear, you'll usually have an offer within a few hours.
    • Pickup turnaround: typically within 72 hours of an accepted offer, scheduled around your availability. Same-day or next-day pickup is often possible when timelines are tight; we coordinate the truck around your day, not the other way around.

    What slows things down: unclear photos, uncertainty about brand or age, or scheduling on the seller side. None of these are dealbreakers — we'll ask follow-up questions and work around your schedule — they're just the variables that determine how quickly we can move from photos to truck at the curb.

    How to get the fastest offer

    The submissions that move fastest from photo to offer share a few traits.

    • Submit during business hours. Photos that land in the morning generally get an offer back today. Submissions that arrive Friday at 9pm wait for Monday — not because of anything we control, but because clarification questions can't get answered until everyone's awake.
    • Send clear photos of the whole piece, not just close-ups. Three to five photos covering the front, side, back if accessible, and close-ups of any visible wear. An arm's-length shot of one cushion isn't enough to price the piece.
    • Include the brand if you know it. A manufacturer tag (usually under a cushion or stapled to the underside of the frame) is the fastest way to confirm what we're looking at. “Unsure” is fine too — we'll do the research from photos — but adds a step.
    • Be honest about condition. If there's a tear, a stain, a pet history, or a smoke history, say so in the notes. We'd rather price it accurately on day one than discover surprises at the door.
    • Mention the timeline. “Need gone by Thursday” or “flexible, just want it out” both help us schedule properly — same-day pickup, in particular, depends on letting us know it's needed.

    If your timeline is genuinely same-day, text photos to 780-965-1477 in the morning — the SMS path is faster than email for first contact.

    Why fast doesn't mean lowball

    The reason we mention this is that “sell fast” gets associated with cash-for-anything operations that quote a suspiciously low number, push for an immediate decision, and leave you wondering if the piece was worth more. That's not the model here.

    Our offers are built from three transparent inputs: brand tier, age, and condition. We resell the pieces we buy through a curated retail channel — the same site you're on now — so the offer reflects what we expect to be able to recover, minus inspection, cleaning, photography, listing, and delivery costs. We'll walk you through the reasoning on any number, and you're under no pressure to accept.

    The honest comparison: a patient Marketplace listing on the right piece can outperform our offer. A consignment placement on the right piece, in the right shop, can outperform too — though usually after 60 to 90 days and a 40 to 50 percent commission. What we're offering is speed and certainty in exchange for some price flexibility. For sellers whose timeline doesn't accommodate weeks of patient listing, the trade tends to be worth it.

    Selling fast to us vs. listing on Marketplace

    Fast and Marketplace aren't a contradiction in every case — sometimes the right buyer turns up in the first week. But the average Marketplace timeline for a sofa or sectional in Edmonton is measured in weeks, not days, and the friction of running the process is its own cost. Here's the honest trade-off.

    What we typically purchase

    Sofas and sectionals from recognized design-oriented and premium retailers: B&B Italia, Natuzzi Italia, Natuzzi Editions, Rove Concepts, Crate & Barrel, EQ3, West Elm, Restoration Hardware, American Leather, Brentwood Classics, Younger Furniture, VanGogh, and a handful of others. Urban Barn, Pottery Barn, and similar catalogue retailers are accepted on select pieces. La-Z-Boy is accepted selectively, particularly the leather and premium upholstered lines.

    Pieces we don't typically purchase

    • Mass-market pieces from Ashley, IKEA, Leon's, or The Brick
    • Bonded leather or heavily peeling configurations
    • Sectionals with major frame or recliner failures
    • Heavy smoke saturation or significant pet odour issues
    • Large visible tears, severe leather cracking, or structural instability

    For those categories, classifieds or donation through Find Edmonton Furniture Bank, the Bissell Centre, or Habitat for Humanity ReStore are usually faster and more honest paths than waiting on a quote that won't come.

    From the time we receive photos, the full process to pickup is typically 72 hours or less. Same-day or next-day pickup is often possible if you're ready and the schedule lines up.

    How quickly do you respond after I send photos?

    Most offers go out the same day photos are received. If you don't see a reply by the next day, it usually means the email landed in spam — texting 780-965-1477 is the fastest backup channel.

    Will I get less than I would on Marketplace?

    Often yes — that's the honest trade. A patient Marketplace listing on the right premium piece can produce a higher final number than our offer.
