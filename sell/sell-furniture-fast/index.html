Sell Premium Furniture Fast in Edmonton

Updated October 2026

To sell a premium or designer sofa or sectional quickly in Edmonton, send photos to Edmonton Refreshed for a no-obligation offer. We aim to reply the same day. If you accept, pickup is scheduled with you and you’re paid before the piece leaves.

We buy pieces that are all of these

  • A sofa or sectional (exceptional chairs considered)

We buy to resell, so every offer factors in what it costs us to transport, clean, store, and remarket a piece. That means our offer will come in below what a private sale would net you.     What “fast” actually means here

    Two timelines are worth understanding:

    • Reply: we aim to reply the same day. Clear photos and the brand, if you know it, help us reply sooner.
    • Pickup: scheduled with you once you accept an offer. We can’t promise a specific day before we’ve talked, so put your deadline in the form and we’ll tell you up front whether we can meet it.

    What slows things down: unclear photos, uncertainty about brand or age, or scheduling on the seller side. None of these are dealbreakers; they just mean more back-and-forth before we can reply.

    Why Edmonton sellers contact us when timing matters

    A private sale fell through

    A buyer cancelled or the listing took longer than planned, and your date hasn’t moved.

    A replacement has a delivery date

    A new sofa or sectional is coming and the old one needs to be out first. Selling it outright means no listing to run while you wait.

    Renovation or paint start is booked

    Flooring crew Monday, painter Thursday. Pieces have to be out of the room before the contractor arrives.

    Listing the home, need pieces gone

    Your realtor wants the room staged differently or empty before photo day. Selling outright means one offer and one pickup instead of a separate listing for the furniture.

    How to get a faster reply

    The submissions we can answer fastest share a few traits.

    • Submit during the day. If we have questions about a piece, they’re easier to sort out while you’re around to answer them.
    • Send clear photos of the whole piece, not just close-ups. The front, the side, the back if you can reach it, and close-ups of any wear. One cushion up close isn’t enough to make an offer.
    • Include the brand if you know it. A manufacturer tag (usually under a cushion or on the underside of the frame) is the fastest way to confirm what we’re looking at. “Unsure” is fine too; it just adds a step.
    • Describe the condition. If there’s a tear, a stain, or a smoke history, say so in the notes. It’s better reflected in the offer up front than found at pickup.
    • Give us your deadline. “Needs to be out by Thursday” or “flexible” both help, and we’ll confirm whether we can meet it.

    If your timeline is tight, you can text photos to 780-965-1477 instead of using the form.

    How we arrive at an offer

    A quick sale shouldn’t mean a rushed decision. Our offers are based on brand, age, and condition. We resell what we buy through this site, so the offer reflects what we expect the piece to resell for, minus inspection, cleaning, photography, listing, and delivery costs. We’ll explain how we got to the number, and you’re under no pressure to accept.

    Selling fast to us vs. listing on Marketplace

    Sometimes the right private buyer turns up in the first week. For the right piece, Marketplace can pay more than our offer, if you have time to run the listing, answer messages, and host showings, and a buyer follows through. If your date is close, though, there’s little room for the listing to run its course.

    Our offer is based on your specific piece and accounts for what it costs us to inspect, clean, photograph, list, and deliver it to its next owner. In exchange, it’s one conversation, pickup by our crew, and payment before the piece leaves. If you have a week or two and want the highest price, a private listing may suit you better.
