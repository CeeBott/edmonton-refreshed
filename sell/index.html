How offers are calculated

Every offer is built from three inputs: brand tier, age, and condition. A recognized brand in excellent condition within the last few years holds a meaningful share of its value. An older piece, or a piece from a mass-market brand, sells for a fraction of original retail regardless of what it cost new. We'll walk you through the reasoning on any offer we make — no black box.
