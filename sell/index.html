How offers are calculated

Every offer is built from three inputs: brand tier, age, and condition. A recognized brand in excellent condition within the last few years holds a meaningful share of its value. An older piece, or a piece from a mass-market brand, sells for a fraction of original retail regardless of what it cost new. We'll walk you through the reasoning on any offer we make — no black box.

The honest comparison

For the right piece, Marketplace can produce a higher number than our offer — if you have the weeks to run the listing, field the messages, and host the showings, and a buyer actually follows through. Our offer trades some of that ceiling for certainty: one conversation, a firm number, pickup scheduled around you, and payment in hand before the piece leaves. That's the trade. If your goal is to maximize price and you don’t mind the time and effort, Marketplace may be a better fit.

How to get the most accurate offer

A few practical things on the seller side make our offer faster and more accurate.

  • Photograph the side profile. Cushion depth, arm style, and back height tell us the model and generation. A side-on shot from a few feet away is usually all we need to identify the piece.
  • Get close to the upholstery. A six-inch close-up shows fabric texture, leather grade, pilling, and wear that a wide shot can’t.
  • Capture any tags. Manufacturer tags (under cushions, on the back of the frame) speed up brand and model identification significantly.
  • Note the room context. A short line about where the piece lives — stairwell access, elevator size, walk-out vs. walk-up — helps us plan pickup logistics.
  • Be straight about wear. Don’t hide damage; we factor it in transparently. Undisclosed surprises at pickup affect the visit.

If you have original purchase documents, fabric or leather codes, or the original retailer name, mentioning them in the form’s notes field speeds the offer.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do you decide how much to pay?

    Every offer is based on three inputs: brand tier, age, and condition. A recognized brand in excellent condition within the last few years holds a meaningful share of its value. An older piece, or a piece from a mass-market brand, sells for a fraction of original retail regardless of what it cost new. We’ll walk you through the reasoning on any offer we make.

    What happens if my piece isn’t a fit?

    We’ll tell you straight, usually the same day. No runaround. If we can’t buy it, we’ll sometimes point you toward a channel that makes more sense — consignment, marketplace, or donation.

    How fast can you pick up?

    Pickups can often happen within 24 hours of an accepted offer, scheduled around you. We bring the truck and do all the lifting.

    Do you take trade-ins or buy back furniture?

    What we offer is the direct-sale version of a trade-in or buy-back: we buy your sofa or sectional outright — no store credit, nothing to list. If a replacement is on the way, we can usually time pickup around the new delivery so the old piece is gone first.

    Do you pay cash or e-transfer?

    Both. Cash or e-transfer on the spot — your choice, paid before the piece leaves your home.

    What if my brand isn’t listed?

    The list isn’t exhaustive. If it’s a recognized maker or came from a premium retailer like Signature Lane or Cottswood Interiors, it’s worth sending in. If you’re not sure, send photos and we’ll tell you.

    Do you buy anything besides sofas and sectionals?

    Generally no. We specialize in sofas and sectionals — that’s where our resale channels and pricing are dialed in. Chairs sold as part of a matching set with a sofa are in; standalone chairs, dining sets, and bedroom furniture are out.
