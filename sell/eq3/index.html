Sell Your EQ3 Sofa or Sectional in Edmonton

Reviewed July 2026 · Hosted by Collin Bottrell, Edmonton pre-owned furniture buyer.

Edmonton Refreshed buys pre-owned EQ3 sofas and sectionals outright in Edmonton. Send photos for a no-obligation offer, usually the same day; accept it and we handle pickup and pay you at the door. As a Canadian-made brand with a strong local following, EQ3 seating resells reliably here.

EQ3 modular pieces require hardware disassembly that buyers routinely underestimate. Sellers with good pieces often decide a single conversation and a scheduled pickup — with someone who knows how the modules come apart — is worth more than the Marketplace ceiling.

    How EQ3 fits into our buy mix

    EQ3 occupies a useful middle ground: more design-driven than mass-market, more accessible than Italian or American premium brands. That positioning translates to steady pre-owned demand. The Replay and Remi are the strongest performers in our experience — leather Remi sofas in particular.

    The brand also offers custom configurations through their fabric and build programmes. Custom pieces are evaluated the same as catalogue versions — we look at the upholstery, frame, and configuration. Bespoke fabrics or unusual colours can occasionally narrow the resale audience, but rarely enough to disqualify a piece.

    Fabric choice matters more here than with leather. Wool blends, performance fabrics, and leather all hold their value well.     What affects the offer

    • Model: leather Remi and Replay lead our offer range
    • Upholstery: leather and wool blends carry stronger resale value than performance velvets or light linens
    • Frame: EQ3 frames hold up well, but we still check for cushion sag and leg stability before pricing
    • Age: 0–6 years is the strongest band; well-maintained older pieces still considered if the frame is solid and quiet
    • Cushion covers: some EQ3 models have removable covers; intact and clean covers help, missing ones don't disqualify a piece

    How to document your EQ3 piece for an accurate offer

    EQ3’s custom build programmes mean many pieces have specific fabric, leg, or finish combinations that affect resale value. A few things you can capture for a more accurate offer.

    • Model name and configuration. Check under a seat cushion or stapled to the underside of the frame. The tag usually spells out the model (Replay, Remi, Salema, Cello, etc.) and the production date.
    • Fabric or leather code. EQ3’s order confirmations (and many product tags) include a fabric or leather code — e.g., “Lazlo Linen” or a leather identifier. This identifies the exact textile and affects the offer significantly.
    • Leg style and finish. EQ3 ships with standard leg options and offers upgrades (walnut, oak, blackened steel). Walnut and oak typically lift the offer; standard powder-coated metal legs are baseline.
    • Module count for sectionals. Modular EQ3 sectionals (Replay, Cello, Salema) lock together with hardware. Count the seat modules, back cushions, ottomans, and corner pieces — and confirm all original hardware is present.
    • Original order documents. EQ3 emails the order confirmation with model name, fabric code, configuration, and leg choice. These confirm everything in one document.

    If you can’t tell from the tag, send photos of the side profile, the leg, the fabric or leather up close, and any visible label. We identify the model and configuration from photos in most cases.

    Pieces that typically qualify

    • EQ3 Replay and Remi sofas and sectionals
    • Leather variants from any EQ3 model
    • Modular sectionals with all original modules and cushions
    • Custom-fabric pieces in neutral or contemporary finishes

    Pieces we don’t typically purchase

    • Modular EQ3 sectionals missing original locking hardware
    • EQ3 dining, storage, or accent pieces (matching pieces sold with a sofa are sometimes considered)
    • Pieces marketed as “EQ3 style” that aren’t actually from EQ3
    • Severe pilling on performance velvets or light linens that can’t be cleaned or reupholstered
    • Torn or stained cushion covers without replacement availability
    • Frame damage or significant structural failure
    • Heavy smoke saturation or significant pet damage

    For pieces in those categories, donation through Find Edmonton Furniture Bank, the Bissell Centre, or Habitat for Humanity ReStore is usually the better path. If you’re unsure, send photos — we’ll tell you straight.

    Selling an EQ3 piece to us vs. listing on Marketplace

    EQ3 is Canadian-made and consistent enough that buyers in Edmonton search for it by name — which makes the brand work better than average on Marketplace. The trade is what it always is: time and effort against certainty and pickup handled. Worth noting that EQ3 modular sectionals usually need to be disassembled for pickup, which buyers underestimate when arranging a Marketplace transport.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does colour affect the offer on EQ3 pieces?

    Yes. Neutral upholstery — grey, oatmeal, charcoal, natural linen — resells broadly. Strong statement colours narrow the buyer pool and affect the offer; worn or faded fabric does the same regardless of colour.

    My EQ3 sofa came with custom legs — does that matter?

    Generally not negatively. Custom legs read as a feature, particularly walnut or oak over the standard leg options.

    What about EQ3 dining tables and storage?

    We focus on sofas and sectionals. EQ3 dining and storage pieces aren't typically in our buy zone, though we'll occasionally take them as part of a sofa bundle.

    Do you buy incomplete EQ3 sectionals?

    It depends on what's missing. A sectional short one smaller module may still qualify; one missing a primary seat section usually won't. Tell us the configuration and what you have.

    Where can I sell my EQ3 sofa in Edmonton?

    Edmonton Refreshed buys EQ3 sofas and sectionals directly from sellers across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan — Replay, Salema, Remi, Cello, and others. Submit photos through the form on this page and we'll reply with a firm offer today.

    Will I get more for my EQ3 piece on Marketplace?

    Possibly. EQ3 has stronger Marketplace performance than generic brands because buyers search for it by name and the line is consistent enough to evaluate confidently from photos. The honest trade is that with time, a strong listing on a complete EQ3 piece can outperform our offer. Our offer trades some price for certainty, identification across the full EQ3 catalogue, and pickup handled including modular disassembly.

    Do you buy EQ3 modular sectionals like the Replay?

    Yes — modular EQ3 sectionals (Replay, Cello, Salema) are some of our most consistent pieces. The locking-hardware modularity is a real advantage in resale because it makes the piece adaptable to many room layouts. Photos of each module and the original configuration help us price quickly.

    Is there an EQ3 buy-back or trade-in program in Edmonton?

    No — EQ3 doesn’t offer a buy-back or trade-in program. But you can sell yours outright to Edmonton Refreshed — send photos for an offer, paid by cash or e-transfer with pickup handled.
