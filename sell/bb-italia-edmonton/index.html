Sell Your B&B Italia Sofa or Sectional in Edmonton

B&B Italia sits at the top of what we buy. Pieces from this catalogue are rare on the Edmonton secondary market, and when they appear in solid structural shape we move quickly. If you have a Charles, Tufty-Time, Bend, Husk, or other B&B Italia piece, this page is the right place to start.

What B&B Italia is, in practice

B&B Italia is a Novedrate-based Italian manufacturer with continuous production since 1966, and one of the most influential designers in contemporary upholstered seating. Antonio Citterio’s Charles, Patricia Urquiola’s Bend and Husk, and Patricia Urquiola’s Tufty-Time are the most-known pieces — but every model in the catalogue is built to a consistent standard.

Construction is industrial: tubular steel and steel-profile internal frames, polyurethane foam in graded densities, removable Velcro-mounted covers on many models. These pieces don’t fail structurally — they hold or they don’t, and most of them hold for decades.

Because B&B Italia is sold through select retailers (none in Edmonton directly — the closest are in Vancouver and Toronto), the local pre-owned inventory is extremely limited. When a B&B piece comes available here, demand is strong and the resale value reflects that.

What affects the offer

  • Model: Charles, Tufty-Time, Bend, Husk, Ray, and Maxalto-line pieces lead the catalogue.
  • Frame integrity: B&B steel frames don’t warp, but covers and cushions do age. We look at both.
  • Cover state: removable covers can be replaced or professionally cleaned; covers in good shape add meaningful value.
  • Documentation: original purchase invoice, designer label, or fabric specification card help authenticate and confirm the model.
  • Configuration: complete sectional sets with all original modules outperform partials.

Pieces that typically qualify

  • Charles, Tufty-Time, Tufty-Too, Bend, Husk, Andy, Ray, and Maxalto sofas and sectionals.
  • Any B&B Italia leather or premium-fabric configuration in good or better structural condition.
  • Pieces with removable covers and verifiable B&B Italia labels.
  • Decades-old B&B Italia — condition matters more than age for this brand.

B&B Italia is among the rarest brands on the Edmonton secondary market. We currently have a B&B Italia Charles sectional in live inventory, but historically the brand moves through quickly. If you have a piece you’re considering selling, we’d like to see photos.

    B&B Italia buyer’s guide

    Common questions

    My B&B Italia cover has staining or fading — is the piece still worth offering?

    Yes. Cover condition affects the offer, but the steel frame and cushion system are what the piece is actually built on. We frequently buy B&B Italia with imperfect covers because the underlying piece is sound.

    Do I need the original purchase paperwork?

    It helps for authentication, but it’s not required. Photos of the manufacturer label (usually under the seat) and the fabric specification card are usually enough.

    How old can a B&B Italia piece be?

    No cutoff. A 20-year-old Charles in solid structural shape is still in our buy zone.

    Are Maxalto pieces in scope too?

    Yes — Maxalto is B&B Italia’s premium sub-brand. Same buy criteria as the main line.
