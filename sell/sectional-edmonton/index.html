Sell Your Sectional in Edmonton

Sectionals are our most active buying category — and one of the hardest pieces for sellers to move privately. The size, the weight, the module configurations — all of it makes a Marketplace listing more complicated than it's worth for most people.

The form below is the fastest way to find out what we'd pay for yours.

What we typically buy

We focus on sectionals from design-oriented and mid-to-premium retailers: Italian and European brands (Natuzzi Italia, Natuzzi Editions, B&B Italia, Roche Bobois), American premium (American Leather, Restoration Hardware, Room & Board), Canadian mid-to-upper (Rove Concepts, EQ3, Brentwood Classics, Younger Furniture, VanGogh), and select catalogue retailers (Crate & Barrel, West Elm, Pottery Barn, Urban Barn).

Modular sectionals separate modules that can be reconfigured — tend to be the most straightforward buy. Buyers want flexibility, and complete modular sets in their original configuration are reliably in demand. Fixed L-shaped and U-shaped sectionals are also in our buy zone, as long as the layout works for a range of room sizes rather than a highly specific footprint.

We don't buy from mass-market brands — Ashley, IKEA, Leon's, The Brick. The resale market in Edmonton doesn't support those pieces at the margins that make a fair offer possible for either side. We also pass on pieces with broken mechanisms (power recliners, hide-a-beds with bent frames) or significant structural damage.

What affects the offer

  • Brand and original retailer: sets the floor and ceiling of what we can offer
  • Configuration: complete modular sets with all original modules outperform partials or reconfigured pieces
  • Upholstery: leather and performance fabric hold stronger pre-owned value than light linens or velvet
  • Age and condition: 0–8 years from a smoke-free, low-pet household is our strongest range
  • Cushion shape: compressed or permanently flattened cushions affect the offer significantly

Sectional configurations we look for

  • Modular sectionals from recognized brands — Rove Concepts Milo, Restorations Hardware The Cloud, etc
  • L-shaped and U-shaped sectionals in neutral upholstery from premium retailers
  • Leather sectionals from Natuzzi, American Leather, Crate & Barrel, Brentwood Classics
  • Performance-fabric sectionals from EQ3, Rove Concepts, Crate & Barrel, and West Elm select models

    Frequently Asked questions

    How do I measure a sectional for an accurate offer?

    Photos are more useful than measurements for an initial offer — but if you have them: overall length along each side, depth from the wall, and height at the back. Ballpark numbers are fine.

    Do I need to disassemble the sectional before pickup?

    No. We handle disassembly and load-out and bring everything needed — straps, blankets, truck.

    My sectional has a chaise that’s reversible — does that affect the offer?

    Slightly in your favour. Reversible chaises fit more room layouts, which makes them easier to place.

    I have a sectional with a hide-a-bed mechanism — does it qualify?

    Often yes, if the mechanisms works. We don’t buy sleeper sectionals with broken frames or torn mattress bottoms.
