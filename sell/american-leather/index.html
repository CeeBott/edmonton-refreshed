    What we look for from American Leather

    American Leather is a Dallas, Texas manufacturer that builds every piece to order. Its best-known line is the Comfort Sleeper, a sofa bed with a patented platform that has no bars and no springs, and a full 80-inch mattress.

    For stationary pieces, the Tuscany, Carson, and Henley are models we look for. American Leather offers hundreds of upholstery choices in leather and fabric, with its leathers sold in lettered grades. The Carson we bought was upholstered in Haven Heritage, a full-aniline, top-grain leather.

    On a Comfort Sleeper or a power piece, the mechanism is part of the offer, so we test it in person before the offer is final.

    Pieces we buy

    • Comfort Sleepers in any size
    • Tuscany, Carson, and Henley sofas and sectionals
    • Leather and fabric pieces
    • Matching sets, such as a sofa with its ottoman or chair

    Does American Leather buy back or take trade-ins?

    No. American Leather doesn’t run a buy-back or trade-in program for used furniture. It sells through retailers, and its Trade Program is for trade professionals. If you’re replacing a Comfort Sleeper or a Tuscany through a local retailer, ask whether that store runs a trade-in promotion of its own.

    If not, send us photos of the current piece and tell us when the new one arrives. If you accept our offer, pickup is scheduled with you, and you’re paid by cash or e-transfer before it leaves. On a sleeper, the mechanism is part of what the offer is based on.

    More on trading in a sofa in Edmonton.

    What affects the offer
    • Model and configuration, including matching pieces sold together.
    • Upholstery: leather or fabric, and the leather grade.
    • Mechanism, on Comfort Sleepers and power pieces.
    • Age and condition, with condition counting for more than the year.

    How to identify your American Leather piece

    On the Carson we bought, a round metal medallion with a star, stamped “American Leather,” is set into the leather, and a printed label with the same star logo and “Made in the USA” is stitched to the fabric. Photograph any medallion or label on yours.

    Your original order or receipt names the model and the leather, which is the fastest way for us to price the piece. A Comfort Sleeper is easy to confirm: open it and you’ll find a solid platform with no bars or springs.

    Selling to us vs. listing on Marketplace

    For the right piece, Marketplace can pay more than our offer, if you have the weeks to run the listing, answer messages, and host showings, and a buyer actually follows through. A sofa or sectional is heavy and awkward to move, so arranging the move for a private buyer takes real work.

    Our offer trades some of that ceiling for convenience: one conversation, pickup handled by our crew, and payment before the piece leaves. If your goal is the highest possible price and you don’t mind the time, Marketplace may be the better fit.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Where can I sell my American Leather sofa in Edmonton?

    Edmonton Refreshed buys American Leather sofas, sectionals, and Comfort Sleepers directly from sellers across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan. Send photos through the form on this page. We aim to reply the same day, and pickup is included if you accept.

    Do you buy American Leather Comfort Sleepers?

    Yes. The Comfort Sleeper is a line we actively look for. Send photos of it both open and closed.

    Do you buy American Leather in fabric as well as leather?

    Yes. American Leather makes fabric-upholstered pieces alongside its leather line, and we buy both.

    How much is my American Leather sofa worth?

    It depends on the model, the leather grade, the mechanism if it has one, age, and condition, which is why we don’t quote without seeing the piece. Send photos through the form on this page for a specific offer.

    Do you buy older American Leather pieces?

    Yes. Condition counts for more than age: the Tuscany we bought was about fourteen years old. Send photos and we’ll take a look.

    Is there an American Leather buy-back or trade-in program in Edmonton?

    No. American Leather doesn’t run a buy-back or trade-in program, and its Trade Program is for trade professionals. You can sell your piece to Edmonton Refreshed outright instead: send photos for an offer, and if you accept, you’re paid by cash or e-transfer with pickup handled.
