Why Edmonton sellers contact us about a Restoration Hardware piece

The room is being renovated or redesigned

New flooring, a full repaint, or a design pivot. The RH piece is in good shape — it just no longer fits the direction the room is going. A design change is one of the most common reasons a well-maintained designer sofa moves on.

New furniture is arriving and the current piece needs to go

Upgrading within RH or moving on from the brand entirely. We schedule pickup around your delivery window so the existing piece is gone before the next one arrives. For sellers who’d hoped to trade in the outgoing piece toward the new one, this is the nearest equivalent — the RH piece bought outright, on a date that fits the delivery.

Moving to a space where it won’t fit

RH pieces are built large by design. A Cloud sectional or Maxwell sofa that works in a detached home often won’t clear a condo elevator or fit a townhouse living room. We move RH-scale pieces routinely and bring the crew for it.

Not worth the time to list and manage a Marketplace sale

Photographing, fielding questions about leather grade and configuration, and coordinating pickup for an oversized sectional takes real time. Sellers with good RH pieces often decide the certainty is worth more than the Marketplace ceiling.

    What we typically buy from RH

    Restoration Hardware sits at the high end of the North American upholstered furniture market — tailored silhouettes, quality materials, and a transitional aesthetic that ages well. The Maxwell, Original Lancaster, and The Cloud are the models we see most consistently in this market, but we're open to any RH sofa or sectional — the brand qualifies across the line. They also surface often in estate clear-outs, where a larger Edmonton home is being settled or cleared, and we handle those leads the same way.

    RH leather is generally top-grain or higher, and pre-owned leather pieces in clean structural shape carry strong values. Custom configurations — a significant share of what RH sells — are evaluated case by case; neutral fabrics and standard sizes are easier to place than highly bespoke setups.

    One practical note on the Cloud: it's heavy and oversized by design. We handle pickup, but the assembled footprint matters for the next buyer. Modular Cloud configurations with all original modules price stronger than stripped-down or partial versions.

    What affects the offer

    • Model: Maxwell, Original Lancaster, and Cloud are the models we see most in this market; we're open to any RH configuration
    • Upholstery: Condition of leather—whether there is any cracking or animal damage present; any staining or tears in fabrics
    • Configuration: standard sofas, sofa-chaise combinations, and L-shaped sectionals are the easiest to place; U-shaped and oversized configurations have demand but it's narrower and they occupy excess space while they find their home
    • Cushion state: Cloud cushions in particular compress with use — we look at how much shape is left.
    • Age: Under 10 years old is our ideal window, but condition is more important than age and all pieces will be considered

    How to identify your Restoration Hardware collection

    RH rotates collections more aggressively than most retailers, and the same model can come in many configurations. A few practical ways to identify what you have for a more accurate offer.

    • Check the product tag. Usually stapled under a seat cushion frame or stitched into the underside of the back. The tag often spells out the collection name (Maxwell, Cloud, Belgian, Lancaster, Petite Cloud) and the production date.
    • Note the silhouette and proportions. Cloud has a deeper seat depth than Belgian; Maxwell’s track arm is squared off; Lancaster has roll-arm detail. A side-on photo lets us match to the right collection.
    • Look at the leg style. Maxwell ships with several leg options (wood, metal, casters); Belgian has a distinct turned leg; the Cloud sits on a low platform base. Leg style helps pin down the configuration.
    • Cushion construction matters. Cloud uses down-and-feather wrap over foam; Maxwell uses a similar but firmer fill; Belgian is firmer still. The cushion feel tells us which line you have if the tag is missing.
    • Pull any original purchase documents. RH emails order confirmations with collection, fabric or leather code, and configuration — particularly useful for custom upgrades that affect value significantly.

    If you can’t tell, send photos of the side profile, leg style, cushion top, and any visible tag. We identify the collection from photos in most cases.

    Pieces that typically qualify

    • Full sofas in any seat count — two, three, or larger
    • Any sectional configuration (sofa+chaise, L-shaped, modular, U-shaped) with all original pieces present
    • Well-maintained Leather configurations without significant animal damage
    • Fabric configurations without significant irreversible staining or animal damage

    Pieces we don’t typically purchase

    • Pieces marketed as “RH style” or look-alikes that aren’t actual Restoration Hardware
    • Standalone RH chairs, ottomans, or dining furniture (matching chair sets sold with a sofa are typically in)
    • Cushions with permanent compression that can’t be re-fluffed or replaced
    • Frame damage or significant structural failure
    • Significant cracking, peeling, ink marks, or pet damage on leather pieces
    • Severe fabric staining or irreversible damage
    • Heavy smoke saturation or embedded odour

    For pieces in those categories, donation through Find Edmonton Furniture Bank, the Bissell Centre, or Habitat for Humanity ReStore is usually the better path. If you’re unsure, send photos — we’ll tell you straight.

A well-photographed Cloud or Belgian listing can move quickly to a knowledgeable buyer. The trade is what it always is — time and effort against certainty and pickup handled.     Frequently Asked Questions

    RH pieces are large — do you handle the move-out?

    Yes. We bring the equipment and handle disassembly and load-out. RH pieces in particular tend to be heavy and oversized — we plan the route before moving anything.

    How long does the whole process take from photos to pickup?

    It can be as fast as 2–3 days. We try to answer inquiries as soon as possible, and always the same day; if an offer is accepted, pickup is scheduled around you from there.

    How old can a Restoration Hardware piece be?

    No hard cutoff. Condition and model carry more weight than age — a well-maintained piece can still be a strong buy, while a newer piece in poor shape usually won't qualify. Photos tell us more than the purchase year does.

    Do you buy RH chairs or dining furniture?

    Our focus is sofas and sectionals. Chairs included as part of a matching sofa set are in; standalone chairs and dining furniture generally aren't.

    Where can I sell my Restoration Hardware sofa in Edmonton?

    Edmonton Refreshed buys Restoration Hardware sofas and sectionals directly from sellers across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan — Cloud, Belgian, Maxwell, Petite, Lancaster, and others. Submit photos through the form on this page and we'll reply with a firm offer today.

    Will I get more for my RH piece on Marketplace?

    Possibly — RH is one of the brands that performs better than average on Marketplace because buyers actively search for it by name. The honest trade is that a strong listing on a documented RH piece in excellent condition can outperform our offer. What you give up is the time, the staging, the buyer logistics, and the risk of the modular pieces being scratched or damaged during a private pickup.

    Do you take RH modular sectionals like The Cloud or Petite Cloud?

    Yes — Cloud, Petite Cloud, and other modular RH sectionals are some of the most consistently in-demand pieces we buy. The configurations are designed to be reassembled at any home, the upholstery is replaceable through RH, and the buyer pool for them is real. Photos of each module help us price the offer quickly.

    Is there a Restoration Hardware buy-back or trade-in program in Edmonton?

    No — Restoration Hardware doesn’t offer a buy-back or trade-in program. But you can sell yours outright to Edmonton Refreshed — send photos for an offer, paid by cash or e-transfer with pickup handled.
