Sell Your Stressless Recliner or Sofa in Edmonton

Updated October 2026

Edmonton Refreshed buys pre-owned Stressless recliners, sofas, and sectionals outright in Edmonton. Send photos for a no-obligation offer. We aim to reply the same day, and if you accept, we arrange pickup and pay you before we leave.

Stressless is made by Ekornes, a Norwegian company founded in 1934, whose main factory is still in Sykkylven on Norway’s west coast. The first Stressless chairs went on sale in Norway in 1971.

    What we look for from Stressless

    Stressless is best known for its reclining chairs, but it also makes sofas and sectionals, many with reclining or power seats. We buy both.

    On Stressless’s Canadian site in October 2026, current recliners include the Mayfair, Reno, Consul, View, Magic, Sunrise, Admiral, and Wing, offered on several bases such as Classic, Signature, Cross, and power. Current sofas include the Emily, Mary, Buckingham, Windsor, Oslo, and Stella. Older models Stressless no longer sells new, such as the Paradise, are ones we look at too.

    On a power or reclining piece, the mechanism is part of the offer, so we test it in person before the offer is final.

    Pieces we buy

    • Stressless recliners on any base, with or without the ottoman
    • Emily, Mary, Buckingham, Windsor, and other Stressless sofas and sectionals
    • Power and manual reclining pieces
    • Leather and fabric pieces
    • Matching sets, such as a sofa with its recliner and ottoman

    Does Stressless buy back or take trade-ins?

    No. When we checked in October 2026, Stressless’s Canadian website listed no buy-back or trade-in program for used furniture. Stressless sells through dealers, so if you’re replacing a recliner or sofa through a local store, ask whether that store runs a trade-in promotion of its own. Our guide to where to buy Stressless in Edmonton lists the stores that carry it.

    If not, send us photos of the current piece and tell us when the new one arrives. If you accept our offer, pickup is scheduled with you, and you’re paid by cash or e-transfer before it leaves.

    More on trading in a sofa in Edmonton.

    What affects the offer
    • Model and base, including a matching ottoman or pieces sold together.
    • Upholstery: leather or fabric.
    • Mechanism, on power and reclining pieces.
    • Age and condition, with condition counting for more than the year.

    How to identify your Stressless piece

    Your original invoice names the model, the base, and the leather or fabric, which is the fastest way for us to price the piece. Add a photo of it to the form if you have it, along with any Stressless or Ekornes label you find on the piece.

    On a recliner, include a photo that shows the base clearly. On a power piece, add a photo with the seat reclined.

    Selling to us vs. listing on Marketplace

    For the right piece, Marketplace can pay more than our offer, if you have time to run the listing, answer messages, and host showings, and a buyer follows through. A recliner, sofa, or sectional is heavy and awkward to move, so arranging the move for a private buyer takes real work.

    Our offer is based on your specific piece and accounts for what it costs us to inspect, clean, photograph, list, and deliver it to its next owner. In exchange, it’s one conversation, pickup by our crew, and payment before the piece leaves. If the highest possible price matters most and you have the time, Marketplace may suit you better.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Where can I sell my Stressless recliner in Edmonton?

    Edmonton Refreshed buys Stressless recliners, sofas, and sectionals directly from sellers across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc, and Fort Saskatchewan. Send photos through the form on this page. We aim to reply the same day, and pickup is included if you accept.

    Do you buy Stressless sofas and sectionals, or only recliners?

    Both. Stressless is best known for its recliners, but it also makes sofas and sectionals, many with reclining or power seats, and we buy those too.

    Do you buy a Stressless recliner without its ottoman?

    Yes. Send photos of the chair, and include the ottoman if you still have it.

    How much is my Stressless recliner worth?

    It depends on the model, the base, the leather or fabric, the mechanism on power pieces, age, and condition, which is why we don’t quote without seeing the piece. Send photos through the form on this page for a specific offer.

    Do you buy older Stressless pieces?

    Yes. Condition counts for more than age, and models Stressless no longer sells new, such as the Paradise, are ones we look at too. Send photos and we’ll take a look.

    Is there a Stressless buy-back or trade-in program in Edmonton?

    No. When we checked in October 2026, Stressless’s Canadian website listed no buy-back or trade-in program. You can sell your piece to Edmonton Refreshed outright instead: send photos for an offer, and if you accept, you’re paid by cash or e-transfer with pickup handled.
