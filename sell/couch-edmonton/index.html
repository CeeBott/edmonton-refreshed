Sell Your Couch in Edmonton

Couch is the word most Edmonton sellers use; sofa is the word the retailers use. We buy both — same piece, same criteria. If you have a quality couch from a recognized brand and you’re ready to part with it, the form below is where to start.

Get an Offer

What we look for

We focus on couches from design-oriented and premium retailers: Italian and European brands (Natuzzi Italia, Natuzzi Editions, B&B Italia, Roche Bobois), American premium (American Leather, Restoration Hardware, Room & Board), Canadian mid-to-upper (Rove Concepts, EQ3, Brentwood Classics, Younger Furniture, Decor-Rest, Fabbrica), and select catalogue retailers (Crate & Barrel, West Elm, Pottery Barn, Urban Barn).

For each one, the same three inputs drive the offer: brand tier, age, and condition. A recent recognized-brand couch in excellent condition holds a meaningful share of its value pre-owned. An older couch from a mass-market brand sells for a fraction of original retail regardless of cost new. Mid-cases are evaluated on photos.

What we generally pass on: couches from mass-market chains (Ashley, IKEA, Leon’s, The Brick), pieces with major structural damage, anything with significant pet damage or staining, and microfiber recliners from any brand.

What affects the offer

  • Brand tier: design-oriented and premium brands lead; mass-market brands are typically out of scope.
  • Age: 0–8 years from a low-use household leads our offer range.
  • Upholstery: leather and performance fabric outperform light linens and velvets.
  • Cushion and frame state: solid frame, intact springs, and shape-retaining cushions support stronger offers.
  • Configuration: complete sets (sofa + matching chair or ottoman) outperform standalone pieces.

Couch types we look for

  • Stationary couches from Natuzzi, B&B Italia, American Leather, Restoration Hardware, Brentwood Classics.
  • Leather couches in any premium grade.
  • Performance-fabric and wool-blend couches from EQ3, Rove Concepts, and Crate & Barrel.
  • Loveseats and matching chairs as bundles with the primary couch.
  • Couches from premium Edmonton retailers (Signature Lane, Cottswood Interiors).

    Common questions

    Is there a difference between a couch and a sofa?

    No — the terms are interchangeable in furniture retail. "Couch" is the more casual word; "sofa" is more common in product listings. We use both.

    Will you take a 10+ year old couch?

    Sometimes, if the brand and condition are strong. Older couches from premium brands can still hold meaningful resale value. Photos tell us more than the purchase date does.

    My couch has a tear in one cushion — is that a deal-breaker?

    Not always. A small tear in an otherwise good piece reduces the offer but doesn’t usually disqualify it.

    Will pickup damage my floors?

    We use moving blankets, dollies, and floor protection where needed. Damage on pickup is rare; we plan the route through your home before we move anything.
