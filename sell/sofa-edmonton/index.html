Sell Your Sofa in Edmonton

Sofas from recognized brands are the core of what we buy in Edmonton. If you have a sofa from a design-oriented or premium retailer in good condition, the form on this page is the fastest path to an offer. Photos in, offer back within 24 hours.

Get an Offer

What we typically buy

Our focus is on sofas from brands with established pre-owned demand in Edmonton. Italian and European manufacturers (Natuzzi Italia, Natuzzi Editions, B&B Italia), American premium (American Leather, Restoration Hardware, Room & Board), Canadian mid-to-upper (Rove Concepts, EQ3, Brentwood Classics, Younger Furniture, Decor-Rest), and retailers like Crate & Barrel, West Elm, Pottery Barn, Urban Barn all qualify.

The list isn’t exhaustive. If your sofa came from a recognized Edmonton specialty retailer like Signature Lane or Cottswood Interiors, even unbranded pieces with those provenances are typically worth sending in. The combination of retailer and visible quality tells us most of what we need to know.

What we generally pass on: mass-market brands (Ashley, IKEA, Leon's, The Brick), bonded or faux leather, and pieces with major structural damage.

What affects the offer

  • Brand and retail tier: the foundation of our offer range
  • Upholstery: leather, performance fabric, and quality wool blends lead resale; light linens and statement velvets are more sensitive to wear
  • Age and condition: 0–6 years and excellent condition leads our offer; well-maintained older pieces still considered if the frame is solid and quiet
  • Frame and springs: solid hardwood frames with intact suspension outperform engineered-wood frames
  • Cushion shape: compressed or permanently deflated cushions reduce offers

Sofa types we look for

  • Stationary sofas from Natuzzi, B&B Italia, American Leather, Restoration Hardware, Brentwood Classics, and other quality manufacturers
  • Top-grain, semi-aniline, and full-aniline leathers — faux and bonded leathers aren't something we buy
  • Performance-fabric and wool-blend sofas from design-oriented brands
  • Loveseats and matching chairs when sold with a primary sofa

    Frequently Asked Questions

    I don’t know what brand my sofa is — can you still evaluate it?

    Yes. Send photos of the overall piece, if you're able to find any tags, include those. Unbranded pieces from quality local retailers like Cottswood Interiors, F2 Furnishings, or Signature Lane are eligible.

    My cat has scratched the fabric — does that disqualify my sofa?

    It depends on the severity. Light surface marks on leather are often workable and factor into the offer rather than ruling the piece out. Fabric pieces with visible animal damage are harder — pulls, snags, and claw marks in upholstery are difficult to reverse, and we pass on fabric sofas with significant animal damage more often than not. Send photos and we'll give you an honest read either way.

    Will you buy my matching loveseat or chair separately?

    Generally we prefer them as a bundle with the sofa. Standalone loveseats from premium brands can occasionally work — mention what you have and we'll let you know.

    How fast can pickup happen after an accepted offer?

    Often within 48 hours of an offer being accepted, scheduled around you.
