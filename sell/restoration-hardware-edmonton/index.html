What we typically buy from RH

Restoration Hardware sits at the high end of the North American upholstered furniture market — tailored silhouettes, quality materials, and a transitional aesthetic that ages well. The Maxwell, Original Lancaster, and The Cloud are the models we see most consistently in this market, but we're open to any RH sofa or sectional — the brand qualifies across the line.

RH leather is generally top-grain or higher, and pre-owned leather pieces in clean structural shape carry strong values. Custom configurations — a significant share of what RH sells — are evaluated case by case; neutral fabrics and standard sizes are easier to place than highly bespoke setups.

One practical note on the Cloud: it's heavy and oversized by design. We handle pickup, but the assembled footprint matters for the next buyer. Modular Cloud configurations with all original modules price stronger than stripped-down or partial versions.

What affects the offer

  • Model: Maxwell, Original Lancaster, and Cloud are the models we see most in this market; we're open to any RH configuration
  • Upholstery: Condition of leather—whether there is any cracking or animal damage present; any staining or tears in fabrics
  • Configuration: standard sofas, sofa-chaise combinations, and L-shaped sectionals are the easiest to place; U-shaped and oversized configurations have demand but it's narrower and they occupy excess space while they find their home
  • Cushion state: Cloud cushions in particular compress with use — we look at how much shape is left.
  • Age: Under 10 years old is our ideal window, but condition is more important than age and all pieces will be considered

Pieces that typically qualify

  • Full sofas in any seat count — two, three, or larger
  • Any sectional configuration (sofa+chaise, L-shaped, modular, U-shaped) with all original pieces present
  • Well-maintained Leather configurations without significant animal damage
  • Fabric configurations without significant irreversible staining or animal damage

    Frequently Asked Questions

    RH pieces are large — do you handle the move-out?

    Yes. We bring the equipment and handle disassembly and load-out. RH pieces in particular tend to be heavy and oversized — we plan the route before moving anything.

    How long does the whole process take from photos to pickup?

    It can be as fast as 2–3 days. We try to answer inquiries as soon as possible when we get them and always within 24 hours; if an offer is accepted, pickup is scheduled around you from there.

    How old can a Restoration Hardware piece be?

    No hard cutoff. Condition and model carry more weight than age — a well-maintained piece can still be a strong buy, while a newer piece in poor shape usually won't qualify. Photos tell us more than the purchase year does.

    Do you buy RH chairs or dining furniture?

    Our focus is sofas and sectionals. Chairs included as part of a matching sofa set are in; standalone chairs and dining furniture generally aren't.
