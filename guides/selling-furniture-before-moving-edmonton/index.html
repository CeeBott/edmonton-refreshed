How to Sell Furniture Before a Move in Edmonton (Without Running Out of Time)

Edmonton Refreshed · Collin Bottrell

The easiest way to sell furniture before a move is to decide, piece by piece, how each one will leave: sold privately, sold to a reseller, donated, or moved with you. Then work back from the date it has to be out. Whether you’re moving across Edmonton or downsizing to a smaller home, the same planning works, and there are options at every stage, including when moving day is close.

If you have a sofa or sectional from a recognized brand, you can also send us photos before you list and tell us your move date.

Start With Two Dates

The date it has to be gone. This is a hard deadline someone else sets: your possession date, the end of your lease, the day the movers arrive, or your building’s elevator booking. After it, the piece is a problem.

The date you’d like it gone. This is your preference: a few days before the hard deadline, so packing and cleaning aren’t happening around a sofa.

Give both dates to anyone you’re dealing with, whether that’s a buyer, a reseller, or a charity. A preferred date can move; a possession deadline can’t, and the people arranging pickup need to know which is which.

What to Gather Before You List or Ask for an Offer

The same information works for a Marketplace listing, an offer request, or a donation booking. Gather it once:

  • Photos in daylight: the front, both sides, the back, close-ups of any wear, and the label.
  • Brand and model, from the label or the original order.
  • Dimensions: width, depth, and height, and each piece of a sectional separately.
  • Condition, described plainly, including stains, fading, pet or smoke exposure, and anything that doesn’t work.
  • Location: your neighbourhood or town.
  • Access: stairs, elevator and any booking rules, parking, and whether the piece has to come apart to get out.
  • Your two dates: when you’d like it gone, and when it has to be gone.

How to Sell Furniture Before Moving: Plan Around Your Move Date

The windows below are planning suggestions, not how long a sale will take. Sale times vary with the piece, its price, and the time of year.

If you have more than a month

You have time to try a private listing. Price it for its condition, use the photos and details above, and say in the listing that the buyer will need a vehicle and help to move it. Pick a date about two weeks before your hard deadline to review how it’s going.

If the move is a few weeks away

Decide each piece now. A private listing can still work at a price that attracts buyers quickly. For anything you want handled for you, ask a reseller for an offer before you list. If donation is your fallback, confirm now that the charity accepts the piece and when it can pick up.

If the furniture has to go fast

With days left, a private sale is harder to line up, because the buyer also needs a time and a vehicle. A direct sale can still work if the piece qualifies and the pickup schedule fits: tell us the date in your first message and we’ll tell you whether we can meet it. Our page on selling premium furniture fast covers that route. A charity drop-off works if the charity accepts the piece and you can transport it. A paid removal service is the last resort.

Moving soon? Send photos and your move date. We aim to reply the same day, and if the piece is a fit we’ll make an offer and tell you whether we can collect it before your deadline.

Set a Backup Date

Choose the date you’ll change course if a piece hasn’t sold, and write down what you’ll do: lower the price, ask for a direct offer, or book a donation pickup. Deciding in advance means the decision gets made on time instead of the night before the movers arrive.

Confirm your fallback before you rely on it:

  • Donation. Charities, including Habitat for Humanity’s ReStore, accept furniture by type and condition, and pickup depends on their schedule and your area. Check that they’ll take the piece and book the date.
  • A reseller’s offer. An offer isn’t a reserved pickup slot. If you keep trying Marketplace and come back later, ask whether the piece can still be bought and when it can be collected.

Where to Sell Used Furniture Before a Move in Edmonton

A private sale on Facebook Marketplace or Kijiji may get you the highest price. You handle the messages, viewings, and price talks, and the buyer has to arrange the move, so it needs the most time.

Selling to a reseller can reduce the coordination, if the piece qualifies and the pickup schedule works. With us, you send photos and details and we aim to reply the same day. If the piece is a fit, we make an offer based on brand, age, and condition and explain how we got to it. Once you accept, we schedule pickup with you around your move date if we can; our crew collects it in an enclosed truck at no charge, and you’re paid by cash or e-transfer before it leaves your home. We buy sofas and sectionals from brands like B&B Italia, Natuzzi, Rove Concepts, Crate & Barrel, EQ3, and West Elm, and consider exceptional chairs. We don’t buy mass-market brands, bonded or faux leather, or tables, desks, storage, and bedroom furniture.

Donation suits pieces a charity accepts and that aren’t worth the time to sell. Book it early if you need pickup.

Moving it with you makes sense if it fits the new place and you want to keep it. Measure the new rooms and doorways first, and ask your movers to include it in the quote. Our guide to deciding what to keep, sell, or replace before a move covers that choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I start selling furniture before moving?

Start as soon as you know your move date. If you want to try a private listing, a month or more before the date the piece has to leave gives you time to adjust the price and still fall back on another option. That’s a planning suggestion, not how long a sale will take; sale times vary with the piece, its price, and the time of year.

Is it better to sell furniture before or after moving?

Usually before. Selling from your current home avoids paying to move a piece you don’t plan to keep, and buyers can see it set up. Moving it makes sense if it fits the new place and you want to keep it; measure the new rooms and doorways before you decide.

Should I move my sectional or sell it?

Measure the new space, including doorways, stairs, and elevators, and ask your movers to include the sectional in their quote. If it won’t fit or you’d replace it soon anyway, selling before the move usually costs less than moving it and selling later.

How fast can Edmonton Refreshed pick up furniture before a move?

We aim to reply to every submission the same day. Tell us the date the piece has to leave. If the piece is a fit and you accept our offer, we confirm a pickup date with you based on that deadline and our schedule, and we’ll say plainly if we can’t meet it.

What’s the difference between a preferred pickup date and a possession deadline?

A preferred pickup date is when you’d like the piece gone, and it can move. A possession deadline is when it has to be gone, set by your sale, lease, movers, or building, and it can’t. Give both to anyone collecting the piece so they know which date is fixed.

What if I have several pieces to sell before moving?

Gather photos, dimensions, and details for each, then decide each piece’s route. We buy sofas and sectionals from recognized brands and consider exceptional chairs, so you can send those together. Tables, desks, storage, and bedroom furniture are better suited to a private listing or donation.

Where can I donate furniture before moving in Edmonton?

Charities such as Habitat for Humanity’s ReStore accept furniture donations, but each accepts items by type and condition, and pickup depends on their schedule and your area. Confirm they’ll take the piece and book the date before you rely on it.
