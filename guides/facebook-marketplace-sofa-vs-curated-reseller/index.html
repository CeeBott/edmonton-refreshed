Buying a Used Sofa in Edmonton: Private Seller or Furniture Reseller?

Edmonton Refreshed · Collin Bottrell

Facebook Marketplace is a platform, not a type of seller. Private owners list their own furniture there, and so do furniture businesses, including us. So the real choice is between buying from a private owner and buying from a business that selects, prepares, and resells used furniture, whether you find the listing on Marketplace, Kijiji, a store’s website, or a used furniture store’s floor.

Either can be a good way to buy a sofa. This guide compares what each typically includes and what to confirm with any seller.

What Each Option Typically Includes

Practices vary from seller to seller on both sides, so treat this as what to expect and ask about, not what you’ll get.

Private ownerResellerWhat to confirm
Asking priceThe piece as it is in their homeMay include cleaning or other preparationWhat the price covers, and whether it’s negotiable
Condition informationWhat the owner knows from living with itMay include a written description or gradeClose-ups of wear, the label, its age, and any repairs
Cleaning and preparationVaries; ask what has been doneVariesWhat was cleaned or repaired, and how
ViewingIn the owner’s homeAt a store, a warehouse, or by appointmentThat you can see it in good light and sit on it
Collection or deliveryUsually you collect itDelivery is often available, usually for a feeThe cost, the timing, and who carries it in
Returns and warrantyWhatever you agreeSet by the business; some sell as-isThe terms, in writing, before you pay

A private owner can give you excellent information, and a reseller’s description is only as good as the work behind it. Ask the same questions of both. Our guide to buying a used sofa covers what to ask and what to check in person.

Compare the Total Cost

Compare both options on the same lines: the purchase price, the cost of getting it home, and any cleaning or repair it needs. Keep your time and effort separate; they’re real, but they’re not cash.

Hypothetical examplePrivate ownerReseller
Purchase price$1,500$1,900
Pickup or delivery$150$200
Cleaning or repair needed$150$0
Total$1,800$2,100

These figures are for illustration only. Your own numbers could favour either option: a private piece that needs no cleaning, or a reseller whose price is closer to the private one, changes the result. Whichever you’re considering, compare the price with similar pieces in similar condition and with the current price of the same model new.

Photos and Materials

Listing photos show what the seller chose to show, in their lighting. Whoever is selling, ask for close-ups of the seat fronts, arm tops, and any marks, and a photo of the label. See the piece in person before you pay where that’s practical. If you’re buying from a distance, ask for detailed photos and a video walkthrough; they can show condition, but not how it feels to sit on or how it smells.

“Leather” in a listing can mean different things. Bonded leather is a composite made from leather fibres with a synthetic surface, and it can peel or crack. Genuine leather can also crack, dry out, or fade with age and use, so check its condition either way. Our guide to leather sofas explains the terms.

What to Confirm With Any Seller

  • The brand and model, from the label or the receipt.
  • The dimensions, and for a sectional, each section’s size and which side the chaise is on as you face it. Our guide to measuring for a sectional covers your room and the route in.
  • Its condition, including what has been cleaned or repaired.
  • The final price after you’ve seen it, what it includes, and when you’ll pay.
  • How it gets to you, and what that costs.
  • Any return terms, in writing, before you pay.

Which Suits You

Buying from a private owner can suit you if you find the right piece, are comfortable inspecting it yourself, and can arrange transport. It can be a thoughtful choice for a main living-room sofa as much as a spare one.

Buying from a reseller can suit you if you value documented condition, preparation before sale, and delivery being available. What you’re paying for is the work that business actually does and records, so ask what that is.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I trust furniture photos on Facebook Marketplace?

Listing photos show what the seller chose to show, in their lighting, whether the seller is a private owner or a business. Ask for close-ups of the seat fronts, arm tops, any marks, and the label. See the piece in person before you pay where that’s practical, or ask for detailed photos and a video walkthrough if you’re buying from a distance, keeping in mind that they can’t show comfort or smell.

Do furniture resellers charge more than private sellers?

A reseller’s asking price is often higher, partly because it may include cleaning or other preparation. Compare the total for each option: the purchase price, the cost of getting it home, and any cleaning or repair it needs.

Is buying from a reseller different from buying as-is on Marketplace?

It depends on the seller. Both private owners and resellers may sell as-is. What differs is how much condition information, preparation, and delivery each offers, so ask, and get any return terms in writing before you pay.

How much does delivery cost?

Private sellers usually expect you to collect. Resellers set their own delivery fees. Edmonton Refreshed delivers across Edmonton and the surrounding area for an additional fee, typically within a few days of purchase; ask us for the cost for the piece you’re considering.

Where can I buy a used sofa in Edmonton?

From private owners on Facebook Marketplace and Kijiji, and from businesses that resell used furniture, which may list on those platforms, on their own websites, or in a store. Whichever you choose, ask the same questions about condition, preparation, delivery, and returns.
