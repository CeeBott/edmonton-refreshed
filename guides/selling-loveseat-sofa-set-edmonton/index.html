Selling a Loveseat or Matching Sofa Set in Edmonton: Together or Separately?

Edmonton Refreshed · Collin Bottrell

When you sell a matching sofa and loveseat, or a sofa with matching chairs, you can list the set together, list each piece separately, or offer both. This guide covers how to list a set so buyers can judge it, how to price the pieces, and how to choose between those options.

The main constraint is simple: a buyer taking every piece needs the space, the budget, and the wish for all of them. That can narrow the audience for a set. It doesn’t tell you how long either approach will take, which depends on the pieces, the price, and who is looking.

How to List a Sofa and Loveseat Set

  • Photograph the full set together, then each piece on its own: the front, sides, back, and any wear. Use even lighting that shows the colour accurately.
  • Give dimensions and condition for each piece, not just for the set.
  • Say whether the upholstery actually matches. Note whether the pieces were bought together in the same fabric or leather, or are only similar, and show them side by side so buyers can see.
  • List separate and combined prices, if you’re offering both.
  • Say whether the pieces must leave together.
  • Describe access and pickup: stairs or an elevator, the vehicle needed, and how many people it takes to lift each piece.

Together, Separately, or Both

There are three ways to list a set:

  • Together only. One transaction and one pickup, and you aren’t left with a single piece. The buyer has to want, and have room for, everything.
  • Separately. Buyers choose what they need, so someone who only wants the sofa can buy it. One piece may be left after the other sells.
  • Together or separately. Publish a price for each piece and a price for the set, and keep the listing up to date with what’s still available. Decide in advance how you’ll handle a buyer for one piece while someone else is still considering the whole set.

A few things point one way or the other:

  • Your deadline, such as a move, and whether you can keep a remaining piece until it sells.
  • Whether you’re willing to deal with more than one buyer and more than one pickup.
  • How well each piece works on its own. A loveseat or chair can suit a buyer furnishing a smaller room or a second space.

If you’d like to try selling the set together first, pick a date to review it based on your own deadline. If it hasn’t sold by then, consider offering the pieces separately as well.

Pricing a Sofa and Loveseat

Price each piece on its own first. Compare it with the same or similar models in similar condition, using sold prices where you can find them. Our guide to pricing a used sofa walks through the method. Price a loveseat from its own brand, size, condition, and comparable loveseats, not as an arbitrary percentage of the sofa.

Then choose a combined price. Matching pieces can appeal to someone furnishing a whole room, but that doesn’t guarantee a higher total, and a buyer taking everything may expect a discount for doing so. Decide what you’d accept for the set compared with the individual prices added together.

When the Pieces Differ in Condition

Pieces in a set can wear differently, especially if one was used more than the others. A more worn piece can still sell with the rest. Photograph and describe each piece separately, account for its condition in its own price, and let buyers judge the combination. Our guide to what condition grades mean can help you describe each one.

Where to Sell, Donate, or Clear a Set

Private sale. Facebook Marketplace and Kijiji reach the most local buyers, and buyers usually collect.

Consignment. Ask whether a store takes sets, whether it will sell the pieces separately, and who arranges transport. Our consignment guide lists what else to ask.

Donation and disposal. Charities decide what they accept piece by piece. Our guide to who buys used couches in Edmonton lists donation options and how to dispose of what can’t be reused.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I sell my sofa and loveseat together or separately?

It depends on your deadline and how you want to handle buyers. Selling together means one transaction and one pickup, but the buyer has to want every piece. Selling separately lets buyers choose what they need, but one piece may be left. You can also offer both: a price for each piece and a price for the set, with a note of what’s still available.

Is a matching sofa set worth more?

Not necessarily. Matching pieces can appeal to someone furnishing a whole room, but that doesn’t guarantee a higher combined price, and a buyer taking everything may expect a discount. Price each piece individually first, then choose a combined price.

How do I price a loveseat separately?

Compare it with similar loveseats by brand, model, size, and condition, using sold prices where you can find them. Price it on its own, not as an arbitrary percentage of the sofa.

What should I include when listing a sofa and loveseat set?

A photo of the full set, then each piece on its own; the dimensions and condition of each piece; whether the upholstery actually matches; separate and combined prices if you’re offering both; whether the pieces must leave together; and the stairs, vehicle, and lifting help pickup will need.

What if one piece of the set is more worn than the other?

A more worn piece can still sell with the rest of the set. Photograph and describe each piece separately, account for its condition in its price, and let buyers judge the combination.
