Natuzzi Editions

Saggezza Brown Top-Grain Leather Sectional

The Natuzzi Editions Saggezza is one of the brand’s most popular sectional designs — this one in a rich dark brown top-grain leather with a right-facing chaise, the warm-toned counterpart to the light grey Saggezza we also handled. It carries the same adjustable ratchet headrests, deep supportive seats, and slim chrome sled legs. We picked it up from a downsizing family in the Jackson Heights neighbourhood of Edmonton; at just four years old it was essentially like new. It measures roughly 115 inches along the sofa run, with a 91-inch chaise and a consistent 41-inch depth.
