Description

This is Crate & Barrel's Lounge in its six-piece L-shaped configuration, with a power recliner at each end. A button on the inside arm reclines the seat. The wall-hugging mechanism slides the seat forward as it reclines, so it needs zero clearance and the sectional can sit right up against a wall. The seats are medium depth, with foam wrapped in a fibre and feather-down blend, and the cushions are fairly firm overall. Because it comes as six separate modules, the layout has some flexibility: the armless chairs can be repositioned or removed to suit the room. In a cream Mossa chenille with a soft matte texture, it reads quiet and modern rather than like a typical recliner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an authentic Crate & Barrel Lounge sectional?

Yes. The Crate & Barrel factory tags are intact on all six modules, with delivery dates from May 28 to June 1, 2026, so the set was delivered in early June 2026. The label is the last photo in the listing. The Lounge is a Crate & Barrel exclusive and is not sold through other retailers. Every designer piece we list is inspected for construction, materials, and manufacturer consistency before going up.

How does the power reclining work?

The two end seats are power recliners. Each is operated by a button on its inside arm, so the controls stay out of sight. The mechanism is wall-hugging, with zero clearance: the seat slides forward as it reclines instead of tipping back into the wall, so the sectional can sit right up against a wall and still recline fully. Each power chair has a 7-foot power cord, so plan for an outlet within reach.

What is Mossa chenille like?

Mossa is a 100% polyester chenille with a matte finish, a soft hand, and a subtle texture. Blot spills right away with a clean, absorbent cloth and spot clean with a water-free stain remover. For stubborn or set stains, use a professional upholstery cleaning service.

What are the exact dimensions?

Overall it is 142 inches along one side, 109 inches along the other, and 37 inches high. Every module is 38 inches deep. The corner module is 39 inches wide, each arm power chair is 37 inches wide, and each armless chair is 33 inches wide. The sectional comes as six separate modules, which makes it easier to move through doorways and stairwells than a one-piece sofa, and the armless chairs can be repositioned or removed to fit a smaller space.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver throughout Edmonton and surrounding areas, and we arrange delivery across Alberta on request. Delivery is offered for an additional fee that depends on distance and access.
