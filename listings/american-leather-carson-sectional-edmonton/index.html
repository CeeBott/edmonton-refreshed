The Carson is American Leather's track-arm sectional: square, low arms, a tailored base on espresso wood legs, and loose seat and back cushions of high-resiliency foam encased in down. Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an authentic American Leather Carson sectional?

Yes. It is an American Leather Carson, built in Dallas, Texas in July 2015 and upholstered in the brand's Haven Heritage leather. Every designer piece we list is inspected for construction, materials, and manufacturer consistency before going up.

What is Haven Heritage leather?

Haven Heritage is a full-aniline, top-grain leather with a pebbled texture, in American Leather's Grade J — its highest leather tier. Full aniline means the hide is dyed through rather than coated with pigment, so the natural grain and markings stay visible and every hide looks slightly different. It carries only a light protective finish, so it feels softer and develops a richer patina over time than a coated leather, but it is also more absorbent. Blot spills promptly with a clean cloth. American Leather's own care guidance for this leather is dusting, vacuuming, and distilled water only.

What are the exact dimensions?

Overall it is 137 inches wide and 91 inches along the return, 34 inches high, with 38-inch-deep modules. Seat height is 20 inches, seat depth 21 inches, and arm height 23 inches. It comes apart into three pieces — a 76-inch left-arm sofa, a 23-inch armless chair, and a 91-inch right-arm corner sofa — so the largest single piece to get through a doorway is the 91-inch corner unit.

How old is it?

It was built in July 2015. Full-aniline leather on a well-made frame is meant for this kind of lifespan — the hide softens and takes on patina rather than wearing out.
