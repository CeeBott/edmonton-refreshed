This is a matched pair of West Elm Jodie wing chairs in nubuck leather, sold together — and they are essentially brand new. The Jodie is West Elm's sculptural take on the classic wing chair: dramatic flared wings, a deep rounded seat, and full curved arms that wrap the sitter, all riding low on solid oak legs. It reads as a statement piece from every angle — the back and profile are as resolved as the front, so the pair works floating in a room, flanking a fireplace, or anchoring a bedroom corner. The upholstery is beige nubuck leather — 100% top-grain, full aniline dyed hide that has been gently buffed for a smooth, suede-like texture and a velvety matte finish. Leather is a natural product: variation in colour and texture is inherent to each hide, no two pieces are exactly alike, and fine leather like this will lighten, soften, and gain character as it ages. Underneath, this is a contract-grade build — manufactured to meet the demands of commercial use, not just residential. The frame is solid and engineered wood, kiln-dried for durability, with high-gauge sinuous springs supporting the seat, webbed back support, and fibre-wrapped high-resiliency foam cores. Seat firmness is a medium 3 on West Elm's 5-point scale, and the piece is GREENGUARD Gold Certified for low chemical emissions.

What is nubuck leather?

Nubuck is top-grain leather that has been gently buffed to create a smooth, suede-like texture with a velvety matte finish. This is 100% top-grain, full aniline dyed hide — a natural product, so variation in colour and texture is inherent, and no two pieces are exactly alike. It will lighten and soften gracefully as it ages. Keep it out of prolonged direct sun and brush occasionally with a suede brush to maintain the nap.

How firm are the seats?

Medium — West Elm rates the Jodie a 3 on its 1-to-5 firmness scale. High-gauge sinuous springs support the seat, the back is web-supported, and the cushions have fibre-wrapped high-resiliency foam cores. The cushions are attached, so they hold their position and shape without daily fluffing.

Each chair weighs 46 pounds with a 275-pound weight capacity — light enough to move through tight halls and doorways easily.
