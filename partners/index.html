A Clean Hand-Off for Your Clients’ Old Furniture

Updated June 2026 · Hosted by Collin Bottrell, Edmonton pre-owned furniture buyer.

When you help a client choose something new, their old sofa or sectional becomes the last loose end of the project — list it, field the messages, get it out of the house. Edmonton Refreshed takes that off the table: a fair cash offer the same day, paid on pickup, and we handle the removal on your client’s schedule.

Built for Edmonton interior designers, premium furniture retailers, home stagers, and realtors who regularly see good pieces leave a home. If your clients buy quality furniture, they have quality furniture to move on — and that’s exactly what we buy.

Become a Partner
We’re deliberately selective: premium and designer sofas and sectionals in excellent condition — Natuzzi, Rove Concepts, EQ3, Crate & Barrel, American Leather, and similar. We only quote pieces we’re confident we can resell, so your client never gets an offer we can’t stand behind.

The problem you can solve for your clients

Your client just upgraded. The old piece is still good, but now it’s their problem — photographing it, writing the listing, fielding lowball messages and no-shows, and somehow getting a heavy sectional out of the house. It’s the unglamorous last step of an otherwise great project, and it’s the part nobody enjoys. Being the person who makes it disappear — cleanly, on their schedule — is a small thing that clients remember.

How the hand-off works

  • Send a few photos. You or your client text or email a few photos with the brand, rough age, and condition.
  • Get a fair offer the same day. We reply the same day with a straightforward number based on brand, age, and condition — and we explain the reasoning, no black box.
  • We schedule the pickup. Around your project timeline or your client’s move date, anywhere in the greater Edmonton area.
  • Paid on the spot, hauled out. Cash or e-transfer before the piece leaves, and our crew handles the full removal. Your client doesn’t lift a thing.

Why I’m selective — and why that protects you

A buyer who takes anything reads as a junk hauler, and that reflects on whoever made the introduction. I only buy pieces I’m confident I can resell well, which means I’ll never overpromise to your client or hand them a number I can’t back up. When something isn’t a fit, I say so quickly and point them toward a better channel — so they still get a useful answer, and you still look good for connecting them. My standards are there to protect your reputation as much as mine.

Who this works best with

  • Interior designers refreshing a living room, where the existing piece has to go as the new one arrives.
  • Premium furniture retailers whose customers ask, “what do I do with my old one?” on delivery day.
  • Realtors in the move-up market, where clients upgrading homes leave quality furniture behind.
  • Downsizing and senior-move specialists handling households with good pieces that won’t fit the next space.

What I buy

Sofas and sectionals, ideally within 10 years old, in Good condition or better.

  • B&B Italia, Natuzzi Italia
  • Herman Miller, Knoll, Roche Bobois
  • Restoration Hardware, Design Within Reach
  • Rove Concepts, EQ3, West Elm, Crate & Barrel
  • Pottery Barn, American Leather, Room & Board
  • Natuzzi Editions, Younger Furniture, VanGogh
  • Brentwood Classics, Palliser, Decor-Rest
  • La-Z-Boy (leather and premium upholstered)
  • Urban Barn, Sunpan, CB2, Article (select pieces)
  • Unbranded pieces from Signature Lane or Cottswood Interiors

Not a fit

These don’t clear our quality and resale filters. No hard feelings.

  • Ashley, IKEA, Leon’s, The Brick (mass-market)
  • Bonded or faux leather, any brand
  • Microfiber recliners
  • White or light fabric on mid-tier brands
  • Unbranded pieces without premium-retailer provenance
  • Pieces with major damage, pet damage, or staining
  • Standalone chairs, dining sets, bedroom furniture
  • Hide-a-beds or recliners with mechanical issues

Classifieds remain an excellent option for this category.

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s in it for me as a partner?

Your client gets a fast, fair, no-hassle exit for their old piece — and they associate that ease with you. The old-furniture problem is the unglamorous last step of an otherwise great project or purchase, and solving it cleanly reflects well on whoever made the introduction. We’re also glad to make a referral worth your while on closed purchases.

What happens if a piece isn’t a fit?

We say so quickly — usually the same day — and point your client toward a better channel, whether that’s Marketplace, consignment, or donation. They still get a useful answer, and you’re never left looking like you sent them to a dead end. We only quote pieces we’re confident we can resell, so your client never gets an offer we can’t stand behind.

How fast can you move?

An offer the same day, and pickup often within a day or two of an accepted offer — scheduled around your project or your client’s move date.

What do you buy?

Sofas and sectionals from recognized and premium brands — Natuzzi, Rove Concepts, EQ3, Crate & Barrel, American Leather, B&B Italia, West Elm and similar — in excellent condition, generally bought new within the last decade. When in doubt, send photos; a quick yes or no costs nothing.

Does my client have to do anything, or move the piece?

Nothing beyond sending photos and picking a time. We bring help and handle the full removal — stairs, elevators, and tight doorways included. Your client doesn’t lift a thing.
