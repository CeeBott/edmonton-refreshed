A Clean Hand-Off for Your Clients’ Old Furniture

Updated October 2026 · Hosted by Collin Bottrell, Edmonton pre-owned furniture buyer.

When you help a client choose something new, their old sofa or sectional becomes the last loose end of the project — list it, field the messages, get it out of the house. Edmonton Refreshed takes that off the table: a fair cash offer the same day, paid on pickup, and we handle the removal on your client’s schedule.

Built for Edmonton interior designers, premium furniture retailers, home stagers, and realtors who regularly see good pieces leave a home. If your clients buy quality furniture, they have quality furniture to move on — and that’s exactly what we buy.

Become a Partner

Before you send photos

We buy pieces that are all of these

  • A sofa or sectional (exceptional chairs considered)
  • From a recognized brand or premium retailer ($3,000+ new)
  • Roughly 10 years old or newer
  • In good condition (no major damage, staining, or pet damage)

We don’t make offers on

  • IKEA, Ashley, Leon’s, The Brick, or similar
  • Bonded or faux leather, or microfiber
  • Tables, desks, storage, or bedroom furniture

How the hand-off works

  1. 1 Send a few photos

    You or your client text or email a few photos with the brand, rough age, and condition.

  2. 2 Get a fair offer the same day

    We reply the same day with a straightforward number based on brand, age, and condition — and we explain the reasoning, no black box.

  3. 3 We schedule the pickup

    Around your project timeline or your client’s move date, anywhere in the greater Edmonton area.

  4. 4 Paid on the spot, hauled out

    Cash or e-transfer before the piece leaves, and our crew handles the full removal. Your client doesn’t lift a thing.

The problem you can solve for your clients

Your client just upgraded. The old piece is still good, but now it’s their problem — photographing it, writing the listing, fielding lowball messages and no-shows, and somehow getting a heavy sectional out of the house. It’s the unglamorous last step of an otherwise great project, and it’s the part nobody enjoys. Being the person who makes it disappear — cleanly, on their schedule — is a small thing that clients remember.

How we protect your client relationship

A buyer who takes anything reads as a junk hauler, and that reflects on whoever made the introduction. We only buy pieces we’re confident we can resell well, which means we’ll never overpromise to your client or hand them a number we can’t back up. When something isn’t a fit, we say so quickly and point them toward a better channel — so they still get a useful answer, and you still look good for connecting them. Our standards are there to protect your reputation as much as ours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s in it for me as a partner?

Your client gets a fast, fair, no-hassle exit for their old piece — and they associate that ease with you. The old-furniture problem is the unglamorous last step of an otherwise great project or purchase, and solving it cleanly reflects well on whoever made the introduction. We’re also glad to make a referral worth your while on closed purchases.

What happens if a piece isn’t a fit?

We say so quickly — usually the same day — and point your client toward a better channel, whether that’s Marketplace, consignment, or donation. They still get a useful answer, and you’re never left looking like you sent them to a dead end. We only quote pieces we’re confident we can resell, so your client never gets an offer we can’t stand behind.

How fast can you move?

An offer the same day, and pickup often within a day or two of an accepted offer — scheduled around your project or your client’s move date.

What do you buy?

Sofas and sectionals from recognized and premium brands — Natuzzi, Rove Concepts, EQ3, Crate & Barrel, American Leather, B&B Italia, West Elm and similar — in excellent condition, generally bought new within the last decade. When in doubt, send photos; a quick yes or no costs nothing.

Does my client have to do anything, or move the piece?

Nothing beyond sending photos and picking a time. We bring help and handle the full removal — stairs, elevators, and tight doorways included. Your client doesn’t lift a thing.
