Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

At Edmonton Refreshed Seating, all items are second-hand and sold as-is.

Due to the nature of our curated furniture selection, all sales are final.

We take pride in accurately describing all of our inventory and encourage customers to inspect items thoroughly prior to purchase or request additional photos and videos before making a buying decision.

If you have any questions regarding a specific piece, please contact us before finalizing your order.
